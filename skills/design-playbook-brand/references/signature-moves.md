## Moves the brand exemplars share
1. **One idea, many expressions.** The identity rests on one concept that generates the mark, the layouts and the imagery rules; the case study shows the idea before the logo.
2. **The system is the deliverable.** Grids, type hierarchy, color proportions and image rules are shown applied across many real touchpoints (signage, app icon, social, packaging), not as a logo on a blank page.
3. **Type carries the voice.** Many strong identities lean on a distinctive typeface or a custom wordmark more than a symbol; the typeface choice is the biggest brand decision after the idea.
4. **Color in proportion.** A small number of colors with a stated ratio (for example mostly neutral, one signature color in measured doses) beats a large palette used evenly.
5. **Constraints made visible.** Clear space, minimum sizes, misuse examples and a one-color version are designed with the same care as the hero lockup.
6. **Presentation is part of the work.** Case studies use large, well-cropped mockups, generous white space and very little text; each image proves one rule.
