Fixture

Fund one balance in US dollars and approve one run each month. Every person is paid in their own currency on the same day, at the rate locked when you approve the run.

Rates are the mid-market rate at the moment you approve, with no markup and no transfer fees, and the run shows when each payment lands in the person's local time.

Same day means local time: approve by 9 am Eastern on a business day and every payment lands that day.

Bank holidays are on the run: if payday is a holiday in someone's country, the run says so before you approve.
