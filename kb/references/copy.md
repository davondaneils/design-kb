# Copy: copywriting and UX writing

Where to go deeper on words in interfaces and on pages. Read GOV.UK and Content Design for method, Podmajersky for UI text, then the platform style guides for rules. Direct-response sales copy (Schwartz, Halbert, Sugarman, Bencivenga, Todd Brown) is covered by Davon's copywriting skill; this list spends its effort on UX writing and content design, which that skill lacks.

## Start here
- **GOV.UK content design guidance:** the most tested public method for writing from user needs, with plain-language rules you can apply today. Opinion.
- **Strategic Writing for UX (Podmajersky, 2nd ed.):** the best single book on UI text: patterns for buttons, errors, onboarding and voice charts. Opinion.
- **Content Design (Winters and Edwards, 2nd ed.):** the process book behind GOV.UK's approach, for structuring content before writing it. Opinion.

## Entries

### GOV.UK content design and style guidance (docs)
- **Who / what:** the Government Digital Service manual for planning, writing and managing GOV.UK content, plus the A to Z style guide. Content is moving to the new GOV.UK content and publishing guidance site.
- **Best for:** user-need-first content, plain language, titles and summaries, "how people read", and a house style made for low-literacy and scanning readers.
- **Why it meets the bar:** official guidance for one of the largest public-service sites, updated in 2026, and the origin of the modern content design discipline. Already used heavily in kb/research/copy/02 to 04.
- **Link:** https://guidance.publishing.service.gov.uk/writing-to-gov-uk-standards/ (older manual: https://www.gov.uk/guidance/content-design/writing-for-gov-uk)
- **Tier:** 1
- **Checked:** gov.uk content design pages (published 2016, updated January 2026, notice that pages move to the new guidance site), accessed 2026-10-10.

### Content Design, 2nd edition (book)
- **Who / what:** Sarah Winters (formerly Sarah Richards, Head of Content Design for GOV.UK, founder of Content Design London) and Rachel Edwards. First edition 2017.
- **Best for:** the process before the words: discovery and research, user and job stories, journey mapping, pair writing and content crits.
- **Why it meets the bar:** written by the person who led content design at GOV.UK; second edition adds updated case studies and a journey-mapping chapter. Opinion: the founding text of the discipline.
- **Link:** https://contentdesign.london/shop/content-design-by-sarah-winters-and-rachel-edwards
- **Tier:** 3
- **Checked:** Content Design London shop page (authors, chapter list), Open Library record for the 2017 first edition, Inside GOV.UK author page (Head of Content Design), accessed 2026-10-10.

### Strategic Writing for UX, 2nd edition (book)
- **Who / what:** Torrey Podmajersky, O'Reilly. First edition 2019; second edition 2025.
- **Best for:** UI text by job: buttons, errors, empty states, onboarding, notifications; building a voice chart; measuring whether words work.
- **Why it meets the bar:** practitioner book from a UX content strategist, available in six languages per her site; the 2025 edition adds content design for LLM features. Already used in kb/research/copy/03.
- **Link:** https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/strategic-writing-for/9781098174323 ; author: https://www.torreypodmajersky.com/content-design-book
- **Tier:** 3
- **Checked:** O'Reilly pages for both editions, author site (2025 release, LLM content, translations), accessed 2026-10-10.

### Microcopy: The Complete Guide, 2nd edition (book)
- **Who / what:** Kinneret Yifrah, 2019 (first edition 2017). A handbook for UX writers with 300+ screenshots from real products.
- **Best for:** applied microcopy: voice and tone first, then forms, errors, empty states, buttons, and accessibility, with many real examples.
- **Why it meets the bar:** focused, example-dense and method-led. Opinion: the best companion to Podmajersky when you need examples rather than frameworks. Already queued in kb/research/copy as a v0.2 source.
- **Link:** https://www.microcopybook.com
- **Tier:** 3
- **Checked:** book front matter (author, 2017/2019, second edition, ISBN 978-965-572-795-1, the book's site name) and Amazon listing for the 2nd edition, accessed 2026-10-10.

### Mailchimp Content Style Guide (docs)
- **Who / what:** Mailchimp's public company style guide: voice and tone, grammar, web elements, accessibility, translation, word list. CC BY-NC 4.0, source on GitHub.
- **Best for:** a complete, adaptable template for a product's voice and tone document; the "voice stays the same, tone changes with the situation" model.
- **Why it meets the bar:** a shipped, maintained guide from a well-known product brand, open licensed and widely copied. Already used in kb/research/copy/03. Opinion: the model brand style guide.
- **Link:** https://styleguide.mailchimp.com/ ; source: https://github.com/mailchimp/content-style-guide
- **Tier:** 4 (company practice, not a standard)
- **Checked:** styleguide.mailchimp.com home (license, purpose), writing principles and grammar pages, GitHub repo file list, accessed 2026-10-10.

### Apple HIG Writing and Microsoft Writing Style Guide (docs)
- **Who / what:** platform writing rules. Apple's Human Interface Guidelines "Writing" page; Microsoft's Writing Style Guide (brand voice, top 10 tips, A to Z word list, accessibility, bias-free communication).
- **Best for:** platform-correct UI text: capitalization consistency, clear errors ("Unable to load content" over vague apologies), empty states that point to an action; Microsoft for terminology and global, translatable copy.
- **Why it meets the bar:** official docs from the platform owners. Microsoft's guide was already used in kb/research/copy/02 to 04; the Apple page did not render in an earlier run and now does.
- **Link:** https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/writing ; https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/style-guide
- **Tier:** 1
- **Checked:** Apple HIG Writing page text and Microsoft Writing Style Guide welcome page (contents, last updated 2025), accessed 2026-10-10.

### NN/g Writing for the Web (research articles)
- **Who / what:** Nielsen Norman Group's article and video library on how people read online, link labels, errors, scanning and plain language.
- **Best for:** evidence for page-level writing rules: F-pattern scanning, front-loading the first two words, how little users read, error-message guidelines.
- **Why it meets the bar:** based on NN/g's own usability and eye-tracking studies; the KB's copy research already cites a dozen NN/g articles. Not peer reviewed, so cite the study details each article gives.
- **Link:** https://www.nngroup.com/topic/writing-web/
- **Tier:** 3 (applied research, not peer reviewed)
- **Checked:** NN/g topic page (7 pages of articles), accessed 2026-10-10; individual articles verified in kb/research/copy.

### Copyhackers (practitioner, courses)
- **Who / what:** Joanna Wiebe's company (since 2011). She coined "conversion copywriting" after working as a senior copywriter at Intuit; Copyhackers says it has trained nearly 10,000 copywriters.
- **Best for:** research-first copy: review mining, value proposition formulas, home and pricing page rewrites tied to tests.
- **Why it meets the bar:** practitioner with long published record and tested examples; already used in kb/research/copy/01, 03, 05. Opinion: the bridge between UX writing and sales copy.
- **Link:** https://copyhackers.com/ ; X: https://x.com/copyhackers (lead only, tier 5)
- **Tier:** 4
- **Checked:** Copyhackers home and About Joanna page, Wiebe's author page (lists twitter.com/copyhackers as her account), accessed 2026-10-10.

### Marketing Examples (gallery/teardown, newsletter)
- **Who / what:** Harry Dry's library of short teardowns of real marketing and copy, plus a Monday newsletter; his site says 130k readers.
- **Best for:** fast, concrete rewrites; his three rules (make it concrete, make it visual, make it falsifiable) are a good editing pass for headlines.
- **Why it meets the bar:** every lesson is tied to a real example you can inspect. The rules are opinion, not tested. Already used in kb/research/copy/01 and 05.
- **Link:** https://marketingexamples.com/ ; X: https://x.com/harrydry (lead only, tier 5)
- **Tier:** 4
- **Checked:** marketingexamples.com home (newsletter, reader count), X profile excerpt (bio links the site, three laws thread), accessed 2026-10-10.

### VeryGoodCopy (newsletter)
- **Who / what:** Eddie Shleyner, former Copy Chief at G2; short micro-lessons on copywriting and creativity, also a book (207 Micro-Lessons).
- **Best for:** craft at the sentence level: clarity before cleverness, rhythm, openings, and interviews with working copywriters.
- **Why it meets the bar:** working copywriter with a verifiable role at G2; endorsed by peers such as Harry Dry and Ann Handley on his site (endorsements are not evidence). Opinion: best for style, not for UI text.
- **Link:** https://www.verygoodcopy.com/ ; X: https://x.com/VeryGoodCopy (lead only, tier 5)
- **Tier:** 4
- **Checked:** verygoodcopy.com about and hire pages (G2 role, book), X profile excerpt, accessed 2026-10-10.
