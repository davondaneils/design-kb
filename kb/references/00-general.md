# General: cross-domain hubs
Where to look when a question spans domains: the X accounts whose shipped work earns attention, the compilations they maintain, a few newsletters, and the galleries of real shipped products. Read the Start here picks first; treat every X post as a lead to re-find on the person's own site.

## Start here
- **Mobbin**: the fastest way to see how shipped products solve a given screen or flow before designing it (opinion).
- **Rauno Freiberg (Craft, Devouring Details, Web Interface Guidelines)**: the clearest public record of interaction detail done at a high level, tied to vercel.com, an exemplar (opinion).
- **Steve Schoger (Refactoring UI)**: the best single bridge from "looks wrong" to concrete visual fixes for non-designers and designers alike (opinion).

### Compilations and directories

### Web Interface Guidelines (Vercel, started by Rauno Freiberg) (docs)
- **Who / what:** A checklist of interface details (keyboard, focus, hit targets, motion, layout, contrast) maintained by Vercel; the original lives at interfaces.rauno.me.
- **Best for:** a pre-ship QA pass on any web UI.
- **Why it meets the bar:** links each rule to WAI-ARIA patterns, MDN or a demo; already used in kb/research/frontend (01 to 06). Opinion: the best single page of web interface hygiene. Note it prefers APCA over WCAG 2 for contrast; the KB keeps WCAG 2.2 as the legal floor.
- **Link:** https://vercel.com/design/guidelines (original: https://interfaces.rauno.me/)
- **Tier:** 4
- **Checked:** fetched both pages; confirmed sections and the APCA note. Accessed 2026-10-10.

### Design Engineering Resources, by Arun Venkatesan (directory)
- **Who / what:** A curated, community-contributed directory of design engineering posts, talks, courses, examples and tools.

- **Checked:** fetched the page; confirmed sections and named entries. Accessed 2026-10-10.

### Teardown and gallery sites

### Mobbin (gallery/teardown)
- **Who / what:** A searchable library of screens, flows, UI patterns and website sections from real shipped mobile apps, web apps and sites.
- **Best for:** "how do good products do onboarding / paywall / empty state / pricing" before you design one.
- **Why it meets the bar:** only shipped products, not concepts; flows are ordered and searchable by screen text; new content weekly. It also has an MCP connector available in this environment. Opinion: the first stop for product UI research.
- **Link:** https://mobbin.com/
- **Tier:** 4 (captures of shipped work; still verify against the live product)
- **Checked:** home page description and library scope. Accessed 2026-10-10.

### Refero (gallery/teardown)
- **Who / what:** A library of web and iOS product screens and flows, organized by page type, UX pattern and UI element, with font and site views.
- **Best for:** web app references (dashboards, sign-up, paywalls) where Mobbin is thinner; search by image.
- **Why it meets the bar:** real products only; weekly updates; offers an MCP for agents. Opinion: the best complement to Mobbin for web.
- **Link:** https://refero.design/
- **Tier:** 4
- **Checked:** home page and about page. Accessed 2026-10-10.

### Page Flows (gallery/teardown)
- **Who / what:** Screen recordings and screenshots of complete user flows from web and mobile apps.
- **Best for:** seeing the motion and timing of a flow (onboarding, upgrade, cancel), not just the static screens.
- **Why it meets the bar:** video of real flows shows transitions and copy in sequence. Opinion: worth it when the question is about a journey; paid.
- **Link:** https://pageflows.com/
- **Tier:** 4
- **Checked:** home page description. Accessed 2026-10-10.

### Godly (gallery/teardown)
- **Who / what:** A hand-picked gallery of websites plus branding, product, typography, motion, editorial and packaging work.
- **Best for:** art direction and expressive marketing sites, with filters by font and style.
- **Why it meets the bar:** tight curation; links to live sites so the work can be inspected. Opinion: the best gallery for bold web design; check usability separately because the bar is visual.
- **Link:** https://godly.website/
- **Tier:** 4
- **Checked:** home page categories and description. Accessed 2026-10-10.

### Land-book (gallery/teardown)
- **Who / what:** A curated, daily-updated gallery of landing pages, with views by section, headlines, motion and OG images.
- **Best for:** landing page sections (hero, pricing, testimonials) and headline patterns side by side.
- **Why it meets the bar:** filters by industry, style, type and color; section and headline views suit conversion work. Opinion: more commercial and less experimental than Godly, which is the point.
- **Link:** https://land-book.com/
- **Tier:** 4
- **Checked:** home page and filters. Accessed 2026-10-10.

### Growth.Design (gallery/teardown)
- **Who / what:** Illustrated UX case studies (52 listed) that walk through a real product flow and name the psychology at work.
- **Best for:** onboarding, purchase and retention teardowns (Audible, McDonald's kiosks, Grammarly surveys) and ethical use of scarcity.
- **Why it meets the bar:** each study is a step-by-step teardown of a shipped flow. Opinion: strong teaching format; treat the psychology claims as leads and check them against the UX and conversion references.
- **Link:** https://growth.design/case-studies
- **Tier:** 5
- **Checked:** case studies index page. Accessed 2026-10-10.

## Considered and dropped
- **Dribbble and Behance:** mostly concept shots, not shipped products; fails "inspectable shipped work".
- **Large GitHub "design resources" lists** (for example bradtraversy/design-resources-for-developers, about 67k stars): freebie dumps with no editorial bar; designresourc.es and similar directories have the same problem.
- **Awwwards:** overlaps Godly for inspiration; rewards spectacle over usability (opinion). Not checked further.
- **UX Collective newsletter:** solid weekly editorial, but the Medium articles it sits on vary widely in quality; Sidebar covers the same ground with a tighter edit.
- **basement.studio:** a strong studio (Geist typeface with Vercel; Awwwards, FWA and Webby recognition per its site) but its X handle was not verified; better placed in the graphic and motion lists.
- **Revenue claims about Designjoy** from Starter Story, Dealroom, Tracxn: estimates that disagree with each other; not used.
- **Sara Soueidan and other front-end accessibility leads on X:** several have moved to Bluesky or Mastodon; listed in frontend-a11y.md by their sites instead.
