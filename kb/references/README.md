# Reference library

A curated list of the best people, books, newsletters, talks, teardown sites and docs for each domain of the knowledge base. It answers one question: when Claude or Davon wants to go deeper on a domain, where should they look first?

Bar: fundamentals first, then the practitioners whose shipped work proves they have mastered them. Five to ten strong entries per domain, never fifty weak ones. Anything that could not be verified was dropped.

## How entries are rated
Every entry carries a tier from the researcher's source policy (`.claude/agents/researcher.md`):

| Tier | Meaning | How a chapter may use it |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Standards and official docs (WCAG, W3C, MDN, Apple HIG, Material, Google Search Central, schema.org) | Support for a claim |
| 2 | Measured exemplars (`kb/exemplars.md`) | Support, with the measured value |
| 3 | Canonical books and peer reviewed research | Support; summarize, quote briefly |
| 4 | Practitioners with an inspectable portfolio | Opinion unless backed by a linked shipped example |
| 5 | Everything else, including X posts | Leads only, never the sole support |

X accounts are listed because they surface ideas and compilations fast, but an X post is a lead (tier 5). The person is rated by their inspectable work (often tier 4); a claim found on X must be re-found on their site, a talk or a shipped page before a chapter uses it.

## Entry format
Each entry records: who or what it is, type, best for, why it meets the bar, link, tier, and what was checked (with the access date). "Opinion" marks the curator's judgment.
