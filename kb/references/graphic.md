# Graphic design
Where to go deeper on logos, identity systems, icons and poster or editorial composition. Read Identify first for marks, then The New Basics for composition; use the galleries to see current work.

## Start here
- **Identify (Chermayeff & Geismar & Haviv):** the clearest working method for marks, from the studio behind NBC, Mobil, Chase and PBS. Opinion.
- **Graphic Design: The New Basics (Lupton and Phillips):** the composition fundamentals (scale, hierarchy, figure/ground, grid, layers) that every poster, slide and social card rests on. Opinion.
- **Designing Brand Identity (Wheeler and Meyerson):** the process and deliverables of a full identity program, so a logo is never designed in isolation. Opinion.

## Entries

### Identify: Basic Principles of Identity Design (book) and Chermayeff & Geismar & Haviv (practitioner)
- **Who / what:** 2011 book by Ivan Chermayeff, Tom Geismar and Sagi Haviv on how their studio builds trademarks; the studio site shows the shipped marks.
- **Best for:** judging a logo concept: is it appropriate, distinctive and simple, and does it survive tiny and huge sizes.
- **Why it meets the bar:** the studio has designed marks since 1957 and Haviv alone has led over 60 identity programs (US Open, Conservation International, Harvard University Press, Discovery+), per the studio bio. Their published work is the evidence. Opinion: the best single book on marks.
- **Link:** https://cghnyc.com (book listed on https://cghnyc.com/about/sagi). Companion monograph: Identity: Chermayeff & Geismar & Haviv (Standards Manual, 2018).
- **Tier:** 3 (book); studio work tier 4
- **Checked:** studio site and about page, Haviv bio naming both books and publishers; AbeBooks listing (HOW Books 2011, ISBN 9781440310324). Accessed 2026-10-10.

### Designing Brand Identity, 6th edition (book)
- **Who / what:** Alina Wheeler with Rob Meyerson; Wiley, 2024, 352 pages.
- **Best for:** the end-to-end identity process (research, strategy, design, touchpoints, managing assets) and what a complete guideline set contains.
- **Why it meets the bar:** in its sixth edition, published in 11 languages per the publisher; widely used as a course text. Opinion: the standard process reference, broad rather than deep on form.
- **Link:** https://www.wiley.com/en-us/designing-brand-identity-a-comprehensive-guide-to-the-world-of-brands-and-branding-6th-edition-p-9781119984818
- **Tier:** 3
- **Checked:** Wiley product page (6th ed., March 2024, ISBN 978-1-119-98481-8) and Wiley-VCH page. Accessed 2026-10-10. Strategy side overlaps Davon's website-strategy skill; use this for the design deliverables.

### Graphic Design: The New Basics, 2nd edition (book)
- **Who / what:** Ellen Lupton and Jennifer Cole Phillips; Princeton Architectural Press, 2015 (second edition, revised and expanded).
- **Best for:** composition rules for posters, editorial spreads and social graphics: point/line/plane, rhythm and balance, scale, figure/ground, hierarchy, layers, grid, Gestalt.
- **Why it meets the bar:** a foundation text from two design educators (Lupton also wrote Thinking with Type); the 2015 edition added chapters on Gestalt, data visualization and typography. Opinion: the best visual-language primer in print.
- **Link:** https://papress.com/products/graphic-design-the-new-basics-revised-second-edition-paperback
- **Tier:** 3
- **Checked:** publisher page, WorldCat record (2nd ed. 2015), Internet Archive table of contents. Accessed 2026-10-10.

### Paul Rand writings archive (practitioner)
- **Who / what:** paulrand.design, an archive of Rand's essays (for example "Logos, Flags, and Escutcheons", 1991; "The Trademark as an Illustrative Device", 1952) and work (IBM, Westinghouse, ABC).
- **Best for:** the reasoning behind a mark: what a logo can and cannot do, and why simplicity and adaptability win.
- **Why it meets the bar:** primary texts by the designer of the IBM identity; short and free. Opinion: read the 1991 essay before any logo brief. Already used in kb/research/graphic/01-logo-identity.md (R1).
- **Link:** https://www.paulrand.design/writing/
- **Tier:** 3 (primary essays)
- **Checked:** fetched the 1991 essay page and the linked article list. Accessed 2026-10-10.

### Logo Modernism (book)
- **Who / what:** Jens Muller with an essay by R. Roger Remington; Taschen, 2015; about 6,000 trademarks from 1940 to 1980 sorted by form (Geometric, Effect, Typographic).
- **Best for:** finding precedent for a mark by shape (circle, overlay, dots, alphabet) and checking a concept is not a near copy of a classic.
- **Why it meets the bar:** the largest curated catalog of modernist marks, with case studies on Rand, Kamekura, Stankowski and Mexico 68. Opinion: a reference to search, not to read cover to cover.
- **Link:** https://www.taschen.com/en/books/graphic-design/02879/logo-modernism
- **Tier:** 3
- **Checked:** Taschen product and author pages (Muller is professor of corporate design, Dortmund University of Applied Sciences and Arts). Note: Taschen now also sells a combined Logo Beginnings. Logo Modernism. edition. Accessed 2026-10-10.

### Standards Manual (books) and Standards (guideline gallery)
- **Who / what:** Standards Manual is an imprint founded in 2014 by Jesse Reed and Hamish Smyth that reissues historic identity manuals (NASA 1975, NYCTA, EPA). Standards.site hosts live brand guideline examples built on its tool.
- **Best for:** seeing how a full identity system is specified (logo lockups, clear space, grids, color, vehicles, signage) and how current teams publish guidelines online.
- **Why it meets the bar:** the reissues are scans of the original manuals (NASA from Richard Danne's own copy). Opinion: the NASA manual is the best single example of a complete system. Already used in kb/research/graphic/01-logo-identity.md and 04-illustration.md (R14, R15, L6).
- **Link:** https://standardsmanual.com/products/nasa-graphics-standards-manual ; https://standards.site/examples/more-examples
- **Tier:** 3 (reissued manuals); 4 (current guideline examples)
- **Checked:** Standards Manual product and about pages (ISBN 978-0-692-58653-2, 2015), Standards "Our Story" page, Order project page. Accessed 2026-10-10.

### Pentagram (practitioner) and Michael Bierut, How to (book)
- **Who / what:** independently owned studio of 23 partner designers; Bierut's monograph "How to use graphic design to sell things, explain things..." (Harper Design, 2015) has 36 case studies.
- **Best for:** identity systems that flex (MIT Media Lab, "How to design two dozen logos at once"), wayfinding, editorial and campaign work, each with a written project story.
- **Why it meets the bar:** pentagram.com is on the approved exemplar list (kb/exemplars.md, #20); every project page shows shipped work. Opinion: Bierut's book is the best look at a working identity designer's reasoning.
- **Link:** https://www.pentagram.com/work ; book: https://www.pentagram.com/work/how-to
- **Tier:** 2 (exemplar, when measured); 4 otherwise
- **Checked:** Pentagram about page (23 partners), Bierut book announcement page, Internet Archive catalog record. Accessed 2026-10-10.

### Brand New by UnderConsideration (gallery/teardown) and The Brand Identity (gallery)
- **Who / what:** Brand New is Armin Vit's daily review site for logo and identity launches (before/after, applications, reader polls; full access is paid). The Brand Identity publishes daily identity case studies and studio interviews.
- **Best for:** seeing what shipped this month and how critics judge it; finding the studios doing the strongest current work.
- **Why it meets the bar:** Brand New also runs the Brand New Conference and Brand New Awards; the-brandidentity.com is on the exemplar list (#25). Reviews are opinion; the shipped work is the evidence. Already used in kb/research/graphic/01-logo-identity.md (R13).
- **Link:** https://www.underconsideration.com/brandnew/ ; https://the-brandidentity.com (X: https://x.com/arminvit, lead only, tier 5)
- **Tier:** 5 (reviews); the pictured work can be inspected
- **Checked:** Brand New review pages dated through Aug 2026; Typographics 2019 speaker bio confirming Vit runs Brand New and lists Twitter @arminvit; The Brand Identity home page. Accessed 2026-10-10.

### Platform icon specs: Apple HIG Icons and SF Symbols, Material Symbols, Lucide icon design guide (docs)
- **Who / what:** Apple's interface icon guidance and SF Symbols (over 7,000 symbols, nine weights, three scales); Google's Material Symbols (over 2,500 glyphs as a variable font); Lucide's open-source icon rules.
- **Best for:** building or choosing an icon set with one grid, one stroke and one perspective. Lucide gives numbers to copy: 24 by 24 canvas, 1px padding, 2px stroke, round caps and joins, 2px spacing between elements.
- **Why it meets the bar:** Apple and Google docs are the platform standards. Lucide is a widely used open-source set with published, testable rules. Opinion: start from a system set before drawing custom icons. Already used in kb/research/graphic/02-icon-systems.md (I1, I2).
- **Link:** https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/icons ; https://developer.apple.com/sf-symbols/ ; https://fonts.google.com/icons ; https://lucide.dev/guide/design/icon-design-guide
- **Tier:** 1 (Apple, Google); 4 (Lucide)
- **Checked:** fetched all four pages; noted Apple's rule that SF Symbols may not be used in app icons or logos. Accessed 2026-10-10.
