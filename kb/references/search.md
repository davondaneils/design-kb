# Search: SEO and AEO (AI search)
Where to go deeper on being found in classic and AI search. Official docs from the engines come first; practitioners are here for testing, data and interpretation, and AI search claims age fast, so check dates.

## Start here
- **Google Search Central docs**: the only source for what Google itself says it does, including AI features (opinion on order).
- **Aleyda Solis (LearningSEO.io, SEOFOMO, AI search checklist)**: the clearest bridge from fundamentals to AI search, kept current (opinion).
- **GEO paper (Aggarwal et al., KDD 2024)**: the one peer reviewed study on what changes visibility in generative answers; read its limits (opinion).

## Entries

### Google Search Central documentation (docs)
- **Who / what:** Google's official docs for crawling, indexing, ranking systems, structured data, spam policies and AI features (AI Overviews, AI Mode).
- **Best for:** any "does Google support X" question: canonicals, JavaScript SEO, title links, snippets, structured data eligibility, helpful content.
- **Why it meets the bar:** primary source; the SEO Starter Guide covers fundamentals. Used across all six kb/research/search notes. Note: docs say what Google intends, not what it measures; pair with data studies.
- **Link:** https://developers.google.com/search/docs (start: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/seo-starter-guide)
- **Tier:** 1
- **Checked:** fetched SEO Starter Guide (content and next steps sections). Accessed 2026-10-10.

### Bing Webmaster Guidelines (docs)
- **Who / what:** Microsoft's official guidelines for Bing, Copilot and AI search grounding.
- **Best for:** the engine that feeds Copilot and several AI assistants: sitemaps with accurate lastmod, crawlable links, IndexNow, and what Bing calls GEO.
- **Why it meets the bar:** primary source that names "grounding eligibility" directly; Bing's AI performance report is already noted in kb/research/search (05, 06).
- **Link:** https://www.bing.com/webmasters/help/webmasters-guidelines-30fba23a
- **Tier:** 1
- **Checked:** fetched guidelines (SEO fundamentals apply to grounding; sitemaps and freshness; links; abuse). Accessed 2026-10-10.

### schema.org (docs)
- **Who / what:** The shared structured data vocabulary run by a steering group with Google, Microsoft, Yahoo and Yandex representatives.
- **Best for:** exact type and property definitions (Organization, Product, Article, LocalBusiness) before checking which ones Google actually rewards.
- **Why it meets the bar:** the standard itself, version 30.1 (16 September 2026). Use with Google's search gallery, since Google supports only a subset and dropped several rich result types in 2025 (see kb/research/search/03-structured-data.md).
- **Link:** https://schema.org/
- **Tier:** 1
- **Checked:** fetched about page (people, steering group, version). Accessed 2026-10-10.

### GEO: Generative Engine Optimization (Aggarwal et al.) (paper)
- **Who / what:** Paper by Aggarwal, Murahari, Rajpurohit, Kalyan, Narasimhan and Deshpande (published at KDD 2024, per kb/research/search/02) that defines generative engines and tests content changes against them.
- **Best for:** evidence on what raises citation in AI answers: quotations, statistics and cited sources helped; keyword stuffing hurt.
- **Why it meets the bar:** the only controlled study in the field; already used in kb/research/search (02, 05). Limit: lab engine built on 2023-era models over top 5 Google results, so treat effect sizes as direction, not prediction.
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.09735
- **Tier:** 3
- **Checked:** fetched arXiv abstract page (authors, v3 of 28 June 2024). Accessed 2026-10-10.

### The Art of SEO, 4th edition (Enge, Spencer, Stricchiola) (book)
- **Who / what:** O'Reilly's long-running comprehensive SEO reference (2022 edition).
- **Best for:** fundamentals in one place: how engines work, technical SEO, links, local, measurement, building an SEO team.
- **Why it meets the bar:** standard reference across four editions. Limit: written before AI Overviews, so use it for fundamentals and the docs plus practitioners below for AI search. Opinion.
- **Link:** https://search.worldcat.org/title/The-Art-of-SEO-4th-Edition/oclc/1287917547
- **Tier:** 3
- **Checked:** WorldCat record (authors, 4th edition, O'Reilly, 2022). Accessed 2026-10-10.

### Ahrefs blog, Data and Studies (data)
- **Who / what:** Large-sample studies from Ahrefs' index, for example the AI Overviews click study by Ryan Law with Xibeijia Guan.
- **Best for:** measured effects, such as AI Overviews correlating with a 58% lower average CTR for the top page (300,000 keywords, December 2025 vs December 2023).
- **Why it meets the bar:** methods and samples are published; several studies already used in kb/research/search (03, 05). Limit: a tool vendor with products to sell, and correlation not causation. Opinion.
- **Link:** https://ahrefs.com/blog/category/data-studies/ (example: https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-overviews-reduce-clicks-update/)
- **Tier:** 4 for the data, 5 for the conclusions
- **Checked:** fetched the study (author, date 4 February 2026, method, sample). Accessed 2026-10-10.

## Considered and dropped
- **llms.txt guides:** Google says it does not use llms.txt (see kb/research/search/05); most guides promoting it are vendor content.
- **"AEO/GEO platform" vendor blogs:** mostly self-promotional listicles; Google has signalled action on self-promotional listicles (Search Engine Journal source in kb/research/search/02).
- **Search Engine Land and Search Engine Roundtable:** useful for news and dated changes, but news, not depth; use for checking dates.
- **Moz Beginner's Guide to SEO:** decent free primer, but LearningSEO.io links to the best free material across sources, including Google's own.
- **X handles for Aleyda Solis, Mike King and Kevin Indig:** not verified this session; follow their sites and newsletters instead.
