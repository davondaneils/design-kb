# Quiz funnel question design: exemplar teardowns

Access date for every source below: 2026-10-29 (fetched or searched that day). Provenance labels used throughout:
- **[LIVE-STATIC]**: the brand's own page, fetched that day as static text. I could not click through JS-driven flows, so only the first screen or a few server-rendered screens were seen.
- **[TEARDOWN]**: a third-party teardown or screen recording; its own capture date is given where it states one.
- **[BRAND-DOC]**: the brand's own help, FAQ or pricing pages.

No browser session was available. The Mobbin connector refused the request because it needs a paid plan, so no Mobbin flows were used.

## Key question 1: Per-exemplar teardowns (question count, order and type, interstitials, loaders, email gate, result to offer)

### Takeaway
The commercial quizzes (Noom, BetterMe, MadMuscles, Hims/Hers, Prose, Function of Beauty) share one spine: an easy goal or identity question first, demographics and body data early with a "why we ask" line, interstitials every few questions, a "building your plan" loader, the email gate placed just before the payoff, and a result that restates the user's own inputs above the price. The psychometric tests (16Personalities, Truity, RHETI) work differently: a long run of uniform Likert or forced-choice items, no interstitials, a free result, and a paid deeper report. The faith quizzes found are mostly the psychometric kind (spiritual gifts surveys) or very short book-funnel quizzes. None used the full commercial spine, except the Hallow and Glorify app onboardings, which are short.

- Botsi's recorded path: Q1 goal "lose 1-20 / 21-50 / over 50 lbs", Q2 sex with a reason-why, then weight with "we don't mean to pry" before it and "thank you for sharing" after. - Social proof stat: "we've helped 3,627,436 people lose weight", sourced, with a defined minimum result (at least 2%), but dated October 2021. | Source: [RevenueCat](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/web-to-app-onboarding-funnel)
- Expectation repeated three times: "subscribers typically lose 0.5-1 kg per week" (goal screen, next to testimonials, with the timeline). | Source: [RevenueCat](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/web-to-app-onboarding-funnel)
- Event question (holiday, wedding, reunion, with a "no event" option and a date) creates a deadline. A pace question follows. | Source: [RevenueCat](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/web-to-app-onboarding-funnel)
- Loader, then email gate: a short "calculating your plan" screen, then the email ask "about a third of the way through... right before showing the results graph". The CTA reads "See my results", and the screen promises a personalised timeline plus an estimate for the user's event. Marketing opt-in is a separate step: "occasional research, advice, and special offers". | Source: [RevenueCat](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/web-to-app-onboarding-funnel)
- Mid-funnel result: a "behavioral profile quiz" of 10 two-statement sliders with "Question X of 10" progress, then a short loader and a label result (e.g. "Structure Seeker", described in flattering terms). | Source: [RevenueCat](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/web-to-app-onboarding-funnel); [Botsi](https://www.botsi.com/resources/flows/apps/noom)
- Later sections: practical questions spaced out (gym, apps, meal kits), agreement statements that sometimes trigger a reassurance screen ("selective empathy"), lesson timing, a food-colour mini-lesson, a stress mini-quiz with a "stress score" and an add-on offer, then "where did you hear about us". | Source: [RevenueCat](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/web-to-app-onboarding-funnel); [Botsi](https://www.botsi.com/resources/flows/apps/noom)
- Long loader with per-category progress bars ("demographic profile 77%, weight loss goals 0%..."). Some loaders mix in extra questions. | Source: [Botsi](https://www.botsi.com/resources/flows/apps/noom); [RevenueCat](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/web-to-app-onboarding-funnel); asking a question mid-calculation was also noted in 2020 | Source: [Zain Manji](https://zainmanji.medium.com/14-product-lessons-from-nooms-online-quiz-44512d1cae6a)
- Result to offer: the final prediction stacks the goal-date graph with a near-term "5% by August 11" milestone. A "pay what you want" trial price comes next (the choice does not change the later price). The offer is "$0 today for a 7-day trial, then... $98.99 ($11.18/week)", with "Personalized plan reserved... expires in 14:48" and a stress course as a "free enhancement" if the user signs up within 15 minutes. | Source: [Botsi](https://www.botsi.com/resources/flows/apps/noom); [RevenueCat](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/web-to-app-onboarding-funnel)
- Critique (analyst opinion): after the whole flow the user still has not seen the app UI. | Source: [RevenueCat](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/web-to-app-onboarding-funnel)

### Inferences
- Two families exist. **Commercial plan funnels** run 7 to 150 screens, with mixed question types, interstitials, loaders, a mid-flow email gate and a subscription offer. **Psychometric tests** run 60 to 144 uniform items with no interstitials; email is optional or absent, and they sell a deeper report. A faith quiz for men can borrow the psychometric credibility (a gifts or type result) and wrap it in the commercial spine.
- The RealFaith quiz shows the minimum viable faith funnel: 4 binary questions, a result that is really a call to action, and a book offer. The questions are moral contrasts, not diagnostic, and every taker gets the same result. Under the Applica and FTC framing, that counts as a non-personalised result, which is a trust risk if it is sold as personalised.

## Key question 2: Demographics early vs late, length, and progress / encouragement / "analysing" screens

### Takeaway
Fitness and weight funnels put gender and age at or near Q1. These are one-tap, image-led questions that also segment the funnel; MadMuscles makes age its literal first screen. The heavy demographics (weight, birth date, name) come later, once the user has invested, each with a reason line. Telehealth and haircare lead with a goal and push date of birth and email to the back half. Every commercial exemplar shows progress (bars, "X of N", named sections) and at least one "building your plan" loader. The psychometric tests show progress but no encouragement or loaders.

### Inferences
- Demographics go early when they are one tap, identity-affirming and used for routing (gender, age band picked from images). They go late when they take effort, feel sensitive, or act as contact data (exact weight, birth date, name, email).
- For men 18 to 45, MadMuscles shows that an age band can open the funnel when it is framed as a benefit ("According to your age"). It may also act as a self-selection hook in ads.

### Gaps
- No published A/B data comparing early and late demographics in any of these funnels.
- Interstitial cadence is quantified only for Noom (2 to 3 questions per motivational page).

## Key question 3: What they ask that feeds personalisation of the result and the offer

### Takeaway
A few anchor inputs drive the visible personalisation: goal plus current state plus target plus deadline (Noom and BetterMe graph a dated projection), condition pattern (Hims Norwood picker), environment (Prose zip code), and name (echoed on the plan and paywall). Many other questions exist to handle objections, teach the method, or segment for email and ads. Their personalisation is often cosmetic, and that carries both trust and legal risk.

### Cited Findings
- Noom: "Without a starting weight, a goal weight, and a deadline, this personalized results screen couldn't exist." The event date sets the graph marker, and the email screen promises "an estimate of how much I can lose before my holiday". | Source: [RevenueCat](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/web-to-app-onboarding-funnel)

### Inferences
- Shared patterns across exemplars, as a design rule set:
  1. Q1 is a goal or identity question with a "not sure" escape (Noom, Hims, Prose intro, RealFaith's moral contrast).
  2. One-tap identity and demographic questions early, preferably image cards (gender, age band, body type, Norwood).
  3. A "why we ask" line on every sensitive ask, and a thank-you after the hardest one.
  4. Progress always visible; named sections for flows over about 20 screens.
  5. An interstitial every 2 to 3 questions in long flows: social proof with an exact, sourced number, a mechanism or "how it works" screen, an expectation reset.
  6. A "calculating" loader right before the email gate, and another before the paywall; loaders may carry questions.
  7. The email gate sits after investment and before the payoff, with the CTA naming the payoff ("See my results", "unlock your exclusive offer", "view your custom quiz results"), and marketing opt-in kept separate.
  8. The result echoes name, goal and chosen items, then the offer headline repeats the result; urgency or a bonus is tied to the "reserved" plan.
- Exceptions worth noting: app-native flows (BetterMe app, Hallow, Glorify) take the account or email first, because install intent is already shown (consistent with [Zain Manji](https://zainmanji.medium.com/14-product-lessons-from-nooms-online-quiz-44512d1cae6a), who says Noom forces sign-in before the quiz in the app). Psychometric brands ask for no email or payment up front, except RHETI, which is pay-first.
- For a faith quiz aimed at men 18 to 45: the inputs that could make the result and offer feel personal are life stage (single, married, father), the main struggle (a pain question, multi-select, phrased in first person like BetterMe's), spiritual rhythm now (frequency), the goal (lead family, discipline, purpose), and a gifts or type cluster. The result should echo these back rather than give everyone one fixed verdict, as RealFaith does. This is an inference, not observed in any faith exemplar.

### Gaps
- No conversion data per question type or per gate position from any brand. All performance claims are vendor or agency estimates.
- Could not verify which Hims and Hers answers change the final recommendation and which only change copy.
