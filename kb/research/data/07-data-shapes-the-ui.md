## Conflicting views
- **Where to format.** Server-formatted strings ("$1,250.00") are easy to render but lock the locale on the server and break sorting and math on the client. Raw values plus `Intl` at the edge is the consensus (D7, D34); the exception is text that must match a legal document (an invoice PDF), which keeps its rendered string as an artifact. X, resolved for the skill: raw values in the API, formatting at the edge, rendered documents stored as files.
- **Future events: instant or wall clock.** Storing every time as `timestamptz` is the common rule (research 01). For a future event at a place, a tz rule change (D3) moves the wall-clock time if only the instant is stored. Practitioners split; the skill's rule (opinion): past and system instants as `timestamptz`; future events people attend store the local date-time plus the IANA zone as truth, with a derived `timestamptz` recomputed when tz data changes.
- **Counts.** Product teams like "page 7 of 20"; database teams warn that exact counts cost a scan (D12) and drift. Stripe and Google publish APIs without exact totals by default (D10, D11). Skill rule: no exact total unless the list is small or the count is maintained.
