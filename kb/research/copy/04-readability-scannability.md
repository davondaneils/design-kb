# Research: Readability and scannability

Status key: **C** consensus, **R** research-backed, **P** practitioner opinion, **X** contested.

## Key findings

| # | Claim | Source | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 79% of test users always scanned; 16% read word by word. | Nielsen, NN/g, 1997 | R (old) |
| 2 | Users read at most 28% of words on an average page, about 20% more likely. Visit time is about 25 s + 4.4 s per 100 words, so only about 18% of added text gets read. Half of a page's text is readable only on pages of 111 words or fewer. | Nielsen, "How Little Do Users Read?", NN/g, 2008 | R |
| 3 | Users see about two words of each list item when scanning. Front-load. | NN/g, First 2 words | R |
| 4 | Split sentences over 25 words; paragraphs no more than 5 sentences; no semicolons; active voice; plain words (buy not purchase, help not assist, about not approximately); avoid -ion and -ment nouns; explain specialist terms on first use; use the words users search for. | GOV.UK writing guidelines and A to Z | C |
| 5 | In a legal-language study 80% preferred clear English, rising with complexity. 1 in 6 adults in England have very poor literacy. | GOV.UK clear language | R (secondary) |
| 6 | Experts also prefer plain language. Use under 50% of the words you would use in print, 15 to 20 word sentences, one idea per paragraph. Reading level grade 6 to 8 for general audiences, 10 to 12 for experts. | Loranger, NN/g, 2017 | R |
| 7 | Grade 5 to 7 landing page copy converted best (11.1% vs 7.1% vs 5.3%). | Unbounce 2024 | R (correlation) |
| 8 | Numerals for all numbers including 2 to 9; commas over 999; % sign; write out a number that starts a sentence. | GOV.UK A to Z | C |
| 9 | WCAG 3.1.5 Reading Level (AAA): when text needs more than lower secondary education reading ability, provide supplemental content or a simpler version. | W3C WCAG 2.2 | C |
| 10 | Inverted pyramid, informative headings, bullets, tables, highlighted keywords, summaries. "Get to the point fast", "Use bigger ideas, fewer words" ("Ready to buy? Contact us."). | NN/g 2017; Microsoft top 10 tips | C |
| 11 | Measure 45 to 75 characters, 66 ideal; body line-height 1.5. | design-kb shared foundation contract (from typographic canon) | C |
