# Research 06: Checking a strategy

Researcher, 2026-10-13. Brief: `kb/briefs/strategy/06-checking-a-strategy.md`.

## Key findings
1. **Five-second test** (Perfetti, UIE): a technique to "quickly measure how a content page performs with users"; users are told "We ask them to try to remember everything they see in this short period", then the page is hidden and they write what they remember; follow-ups ask, for example, "What is the most important information on this page?" [F1]. design-playbook-brand 2a uses it for concept legibility with 3 to 5 viewers (opinion) and counts a fresh-context subagent as one viewer, never the builder [F2].
2. **Message testing** (Wynter): "Message testing analyzes a company's marketing message to evaluate how well it resonates with its target audience" on clarity, relevance, value and differentiation; "The standard is that it takes 12-13 responses to reach saturation" [F3]. Wynter's panel is LinkedIn-verified B2B professionals (search summary; panel size not verified on the page) [F4].
3. **Hide-the-logo test** is already design-director step 11: "hide the logo; could the visuals belong to a competitor? If yes, the art direction failed." [F5]
4. **Competitor wall of marks**: design-graphic 01, "12 to 20 category marks, all grayscale at 48 px. If yours does not pop out or could be swapped with one, it fails distinctiveness. (opinion)" [F6].
5. **Swap test on words**: research 03 C10.
6. **Claims must be held as evidence before publishing**: FTC reasonable basis; CAP 3.7 (research 03 C12, C13). The design system's `claims_inventory.mjs` checks numbers and dates against the page; FOUNDATIONS row 16 requires every claim ticked against its source [F7].
7. **Recognition beats liking as the test of an identity** (research 05 E2, Caruso et al. 2025 as cited).

## Conflicting views
- Small-n tests versus surveys: saturation figures come from qualitative research (Wynter cites "9 to 17 participants can be sufficient") [F3]; they find problems, they do not estimate shares. Fame and uniqueness need a survey of category buyers (research 05).
