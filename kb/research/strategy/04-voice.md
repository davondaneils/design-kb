# Research 04: Voice

Researcher, 2026-10-13. Brief: `kb/briefs/strategy/04-voice.md`. This note maps existing material; the deep voice research lives in `kb/research/copy/` and Davon's crafting-voice-identity skill.

## Key findings
1. **Voice is constant, tone moves.** Mailchimp: "You have the same voice all the time, but your tone changes." [D1]
2. **The "this, not that" pair is the working unit.** Mailchimp: "We're weird but not inappropriate, smart but not snobbish." [D1] crafting-voice-identity calls it the Polarity Principle: "We are witty, but not sarcastic." [D2] design-copy 03 builds 3 to 4 two-sided attributes because "Two-sided pairs stop over-steering" [D3]. website-strategy stage 5: "Define voice by what it is and what it is not." [D4]
3. **A voice is decision rules, not adjectives** (Podmajersky's voice chart, via design-copy 03) [D3]; crafting-voice-identity adds a four-spectrum map and a competitor tonality analysis of "2-3 of your key competitors" [D2].
4. **Clarity outranks personality** and some moments take no humor: design-copy 03 fundamentals ("No humor in errors, money loss, security, health, legal or bereavement moments") [D3].
5. **Typeface persona is perceived.** Brumberger (2003, Technical Communication 50(2)): studies "provide support for the idea that readers ascribe personality attributes both to typefaces and to text passages"; the companion study found readers recognize typeface appropriateness (bibliographic abstract; full text not accessed) [D5]. Supports pairing each voice attribute with a type decision (research 05).
