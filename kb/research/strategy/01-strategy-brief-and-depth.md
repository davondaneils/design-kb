# Research 01: The strategy brief and how deep to go

Researcher, 2026-10-13. Brief: `kb/briefs/strategy/01-strategy-brief-and-depth.md`. Fetched with Parallel Search and WebFetch on 2026-10-13 unless marked. Status column: C consensus, R research, P practitioner, X contested.

## Key findings
1. **The full brand process has five phases**: conducting research, clarifying strategy, designing identity, creating touchpoints, managing assets (Wheeler, every edition since the third; table of contents of the third edition and a Wiley-published summary) [A1][A2]. Strategy is phase 2 and is short relative to the whole: in the third edition table of contents it spans pages 116 to 122 of a 90 to 178 page process section [A1]. Opinion: strategy is a narrow, decisive step, not a document genre.
2. **Wheeler's strategy phase is about narrowing.** AIGA Raleigh's recap of her talk: "Narrow the focus. Brands get stronger as you narrow the focus." and the phase ends in "a brand brief" [A3].
3. **The design system already demands a short strategy for small jobs.** design-playbook-brand section 0: for a campaign, event or one-off artifact, "write three lines instead (audience, the one true fact the idea comes from, the competitor wall you checked)" [A4]. The held-out test showed the cost of over-scoping: the playbook "demands website-strategy and a full guidelines page before the mark; on a poster-plus-site brief that is scope the brief did not ask for" [A5].
4. **website-strategy is built for a client proposal.** Its own description: "End-to-end website strategy framework for freelance proposals and client projects" with six stages ending in "proposal/build brief" [A6]. Its stage 5 says "A designer or developer reading it should be able to make every decision without asking a question" [A6]. That goal is shared; the output length is not.
5. **Each downstream skill already asks for a strategy input**: art direction needs a true fact ("Positioning comes from website-strategy") [A7]; design-color needs the competitor set [A8]; design-copy 03 takes "brand positioning and personality inputs" from website-strategy [A9]. A single brief removes three separate asks.

## Numbers
- Depth tiers (opinion, from A4 and A5): inherit (0 new lines), three lines, one page (about 400 to 600 words, opinion), full (website-strategy stages 2 to 5 as inputs, then the one-page brief as the summary).
- Wheeler phases: 5.

## Conflicting views
- website-strategy says "Work through the stages in order. Each one feeds the next" [A6]; design-playbook-brand section 0 says strategy depth follows the brief [A4]. Resolution (opinion): run all stages only for full-depth jobs; for others, take the fields the brief needs.
