# Research 07: How top practitioners pick a brand typeface (method, not lists)

Raised by a live job (Flagship Build Co., 2026-10-14). The owner rejected three rounds of pairings as "grotesks and monos, they're all so similar" and asked how the faces were being picked. Researcher pass by the agency thread, 2026-10-14. Status key: **C** consensus, **X** contested, **O** opinion, **M** measured or observed here.

Finding in one line: the agent was choosing by category from fonts it could download, which produces safe, interchangeable pairs. Practitioners start from the brand's concept and voice, trace the letterform lineage that fits that story, pair two faces so that neither carries the brand alone, check whether the result is ownable, and only then set tight display spacing.

## Diagnosis of the failed rounds (M)
- Candidates were drawn from free Fontsource and Google families the agent could install, then sorted by category (grotesk, mono, humanist). Category was the first filter, concept was never one.
- Every pairing differed on one weak axis (grotesk plus mono), so all six boards shared one texture. The selection rule "contrast on one axis" was met on paper and failed to the eye, because the axis was too small to read.
- Headline settings were timid: tracking about -0.02em, leading 1.04 to 1.1. Premium display practice runs tighter (S2).
- Commercial foundries were never considered, so the brief's "quiet earned prestige" was being matched against the most installed fonts on the web.

Notes on the sources:

## What this changes in the skill
- design-typography 02 gains a "Brand type selection method" ahead of the starting-points table: concept and voice words first, lineage second, a tension pair third, an ownability check fourth, display settings last (S1, S3, S4, S5).
- "Contrast on one axis" now needs a visible axis. Grotesk plus mono or grotesk plus grotesk fails the squint test for a brand; it is fine for product UI.
- Budget becomes a fundamentals question for brand work: ask whether a commercial license is available before shortlisting, because the answer changes the candidate pool (S5, S6).
- Display settings: start headline tracking at -2% and test to -4% on large display; take leading to the FOUNDATIONS floor (0.95 at 100px and up, 1.1 below) before proposing any deviation (S2, opinion).
- Show three directions that differ in concept, not in category. Each board names its voice in three words and its lineage in one line.
