## 3. Ranked shortfalls

Fundamentals first, then expert gaps. "Said and ignored" means a skill line covers it; "silent" means none does; "wrong" means a skill line produces the defect.

### Fundamentals (gate)

1. **Sentences about the plan go false when the plan moves (gate 16c, d, g).**
   - Measured: PageUp to 12:45 gives "Your out leg crosses up to 15 kn of wind against tide"; the leg meets 12 kn. The figure is wrong for 33 departures (wind against tide) and 53 (gusts). A return at 16:00 reads "stream against you" with the stream fair at 0.2 kn (18 departures). The lead sentence "The stream is with you both ways" contradicts the page's own north-south route against an east-west stream.
   - Skill: `skills/design-playbook-dashboard/SKILL.md` line 38 (state truth) requires the answer sentence to be recomputed, which the builder did, and is **silent** on scope: a claim about the person's choice must quote the choice's own exposure, not the period's peak. `skills/design-copy/references/06-copy-in-context.md` is **silent** on three-way states (with, slack, against: a binary label turns slack into a falsehood) and on sample models: "sample" licenses invented numbers, not an impossible physical relation. `tools/lint-truth.mjs` drives live ticks, not slider input: **silent**. Fix: in the playbook, "a sentence about what the person chose quotes what that choice meets ('your out leg meets 12 kn of wind against tide from 13:21'); the period's peak belongs to the warning title"; in design-copy 06, "a state with a neutral middle (slack water, flat change, on budget) gets a word for the middle", and "sample data is invented, never impossible: a model a domain expert would reject fails truth even when labelled sample"; in the director's loop, an operate step that walks every `role="slider"` through its range with arrow keys and re-runs `claims_inventory.mjs --render` at five points.
   - Brief note for the editor-in-chief: Brief 2 asks for "the departure window when the tide is with you" on a crossing where the tide is across. The builder should have modelled set (or chosen a stream station on the track), but the brief invited the error.

2. **The plan lane moves under the pointer during a drag (gate 15).**
   - Measured: 69px at 390, 768 and 1024; 90px at 1440. Mid-drag at 1440 the handle leaves the pointer by 21px at 12:40, when the answer line gains a sentence and the facts notes wrap.
   - Skill: `skills/design-polish/references/06-polish-checklist.md` line 26 (no layout shift on state change) covers hover, focus, error, selected and loading: **silent** on text that recomputes above a control being manipulated. `skills/design-motion/references/04-micro-interactions.md` line 33 ("Track 1:1 while the pointer is down") is **said and ignored** in effect: 1:1 in x, 21 to 90px off in y. Lint `state-shift` compares `?state=` URLs only: **silent**. Fix: polish rule "on a direct-manipulation screen nothing above the control changes height: reserve the answer and notes at their longest line count (`min-block-size` in `lh`), or put the live sentence below the control"; lint pass that focuses every `role="slider"`, steps it across its range and fails if its top moves more than 1px.

3. **Number and unit, and ranges, break across lines (gate 16a, b).**
   - Measured: "20 / kn" at 768, 1024 and 1440; "12 / kn", "18 / kn", "34 / kn" and "10:05 / to 12:35", "16:15 / to 17:10" at 390 (`critic/static-probe.txt`).
   - Skill: `skills/design-copy/references/06-copy-in-context.md` line 11 ("No split units") and FOUNDATIONS content mechanics: **said and ignored**; every such string is built in JS (`plan.js` `fmtIn`, warning bodies) with plain spaces. The rule is `[judged]` and no tool checks it: **silent**. Fix: add a lint check `unit-break` (walk text nodes, match a number plus unit, a range or a date, fail when the match's client rects sit on two lines; `critic/static-probe.mjs` is a working draft); design-frontend `references/06-design-to-code.md` line 43 should add "format helpers emit U+00A0, because generated strings never pass through a hand edit".

4. **The phone loses the return times (gate 1, WCAG 1.4.10; gate 16f).**
   - Measured: Back header right edge 431 against a clip edge at 374; four times unreachable at 390.
   - Skill: `skills/design-product-ui/references/01-tables.md` line 14 ("If the table scrolls, it scrolls inside its own labelled, focusable region") and line 43: **said and ignored**. Lint `phone-facts` checks presence in the DOM, `overflow` checks the page: both **silent** on content clipped by an `overflow: clip|hidden` ancestor. Fix: wrap the table in the same `.table-wrap` region the hourly table uses, or reflow it to two lines per waypoint at phone width; lint fails any table cell whose rect crosses a non-scrolling clipping ancestor.

5. **Handle states and the sheet (gate 15, weak).** Hover and dragging fill navy-50 against white, 1.07:1, ring 1.5 to 2px. `skills/design-polish/references/01-interactive-states.md` line 35 (dragged 16% state layer): **said and ignored**. The state sheet shows three captions over one look; the lint `state-sheet` does not compare cells: **silent** (same class as round 2's ghost "pressed").

### Expert gaps (above the gate)

6. **The red is on the wrong warning.** Warning 1's badge and hatch are danger red while its row says "Clear of your plan"; the two warnings that do affect the return are brown-amber. Severity of the period and effect on the plan are two facts painted as one. Fix (opinion): the badge and the row tone follow the effect on the plan; the period's severity stays in the hatch and the title. Skill: `skills/design-layout/references/06-pacing-signature-and-phone-editing.md` line 73 ("one fact is painted once") is the nearest line; nothing names severity versus effect: **silent**.

7. **Warnings are not in the first screen at 1440, and rows are cards in all but name.** Three rows at 110 to 132px against the skill's "one row each from lg (about 55px measured)" and "one line each" (`design-layout/references/06` lines 52 and 73): **said and ignored** (each row stacks title, a 52ch body and an effect line). The effect line is the decision and could be the whole row at rest, with the body behind the hover that already outlines the span.

8. **The title outweighs the decision.** h1 25/600 equals the plan figures; the crumb already names the route. Linear's issue title is 20 (`linear-app.md`). "Passage 4.5 nm", a constant, takes a quarter of the facts strip at the same weight as the two times a sailor chooses. Fix (opinion): h1 at 20, the distance into the page sub line, and the freed cell for "Home 17:31", the number the third warning is about. `skills/design-typography/references/04-hierarchy-ui-editorial.md`: **silent** on title against answer figures on a single-object screen.

9. **The tide height row has no job.** Nothing on the page is derived from height: no draft, no clearance at the Lymington river or Yarmouth harbour, no sill. A sailor with a 1.5m keel reads LW 0.7 m at 15:30 as the first question, and the page never answers it. ## 4. Would Linear's or Things' team ship this?

Not yet, and for reasons of truth, not taste. This is the best-conceived app screen in the tests so far: an answer-first verdict, a timeline that puts tide, stream, wind and daylight against the passage on one axis with the legs carried up through every row, warnings numbered to their spans, a partial failure that stays in its own row, and copy a sailor would recognise as written by a sailor ("reef before you leave Yarmouth"). Things' team would admire the calm; Linear's would admire the restraint of the motion. Both would stop at the same place: the screen's one control makes it lie. Drag a departure and the lane jumps away from your hand by up to 90px while the sentence above it quotes 15 kn of wind against tide your leg never meets, calls a fair slack "against you", and opens with "the stream is with you both ways" over a map that shows you crossing it. On a phone the return times are cut off and "20 kn" breaks in half. Fix the four gate classes (reserve the text height, scope every claim to the leg, model the stream across the track, add no-break spaces and a scroll region) and the screen is close. To reach Linear it then needs to spend its colour only on what affects the plan, put the warnings in the first screen as one-line rows, and make the map answer a question by moving with the timeline.
