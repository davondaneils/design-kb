## 4. Ranked shortfalls, with skill attribution

Fundamentals first (gate fails, then near-misses), then expert gaps. "Ignored" means the skill says it and the build did not do it. "Silent" or "wrong" means the skill would not have prevented it.

### Gate fails
1. **Selected segment under 3:1 (item 3).** Measured 1.21:1 (light) and 1.08:1 (dark) fill against the track, with the same weight on both segments and a label ink delta of 2.26:1 and 1.68:1. Exemplar: Vercel marks the active nav with a fill *and* weight 600 vs 500 (teardown "Sidebar"). Stripe marks the active item with 700 vs 400 plus color (teardown "Type"). **Attribution:** the skill says it and the builder ignored it: `skills/design-color/references/contrast-accessibility.md` line 57 ("Selected tabs ... each need their own check") and `skills/design-foundations/references/accessibility.md` line 37 (states 3:1). The skill is also **wrong/loose** in `skills/design-product-ui/references/04-shells-density.md` line 9 and `SKILL.md` line 34. They accept "a non-color cue (weight or a selected surface)" with no contrast number, which blesses exactly this 1.08:1 surface. Fix there: "a selected surface counts only at 3:1 against its neighbor; otherwise add weight +100 and an ink step, or a 2px indicator".
2. **State truth after Approve and in error (item 16).** The four contradictions are listed in the gate row. Exemplar: Stripe pairs every number with its basis (`stripe-dashboard.md` via playbook non-negotiable 12). Linear states status in words that change with the state (teardown "Copy and UX"). **Attribution:** the skill says it and the builder ignored it: `skills/design-playbook-dashboard/SKILL.md` line 38 (non-negotiable 10, "Every number is true in every state and after every live update") and line 35 (non-negotiable 7, mark every affected number). There is also a **tool gap**: `tools/lint-truth.mjs` exercises `data-source` ticks, not user actions, so an Approve click that leaves "estimates until you approve" on screen passes the lint. The playbook's "Done means" (line 46) should require a `?state=approved` (post-action) URL for every primary action. The label "People paid" for a not-yet-paid run is **silent** in `skills/design-copy/references/02-ux-microcopy.md`: no rule says KPI labels must be true in every state ("People in this run").

### Near-misses on basics
3. **12px data on phone.** At 390, 48% of characters are 12px, including status words and the per-currency rate line. Exemplars are 15 to 16px body at 390 (Stripe, Vercel teardown "Phone (390)"). **Attribution:** the skill says it, the builder ignored it, and the skill contradicts itself. `skills/design-product-ui/SKILL.md` line 35 says "UI text stays 14px or more", but line 51 allows "headers and meta `--text-xs` 12". `references/01-tables.md` line 54 (phone list item) gives height and padding but no type size. Fix: "on phones, line 2 of a collapsed row is 14px; 12px only for column headers and timestamps".
4. **No sticky table header on a 1,824px table.** The 38-row people table (48px rows) scrolls its header away. Only the top bar and sidebar are sticky (computed `position`). **Attribution:** the skill says it, the builder ignored it: `skills/design-product-ui/SKILL.md` line 68 ("Sticky header whenever the table is taller than its viewport").
5. **Phone row target is 109x24 inside an 89px row.** The row hover tint promises a big target that does not exist, and no row click forwards to the link. The lint warns 38 times. **Attribution:** the skill is **wrong**: `skills/design-product-ui/references/01-tables.md` line 68 ("row hover tints the whole row so the target feels large") teaches a perceived target. It should say "stretch the primary link over the row (`::after { inset: 0 }` on the cell link) so the row *is* the target, 44px+ on touch".
6. **The total is not the dominant number, and the answer line precedes it.** All four KPI values are 31px/600. The 16px sentence "Up $3,033.49 from September..." is read before the $265,195.92 it explains, and the delta then appears a second time under the total. **Attribution:** the skill is **silent/wrong** for decision screens. `skills/design-product-ui/references/06-dashboards.md` line 28 and `skills/design-layout/references/05-page-composition-and-density.md` line 30 give *every* tile "value (largest text on the page)", and line 26 orders "answer line, KPI row" for analytical dashboards only. Nothing covers an approval screen, where one number is the object of the decision. Needed rule: "On a review-and-commit screen the committed amount is the single largest figure (39px), the counts drop to 20px, and the explanation sits under it".
7. **KPIs stack to one column at 768.** 4 rows, about 320px, where the skill says 2x2. **Attribution:** the skill says it, the builder ignored it: `skills/design-layout/references/05-page-composition-and-density.md` line 35.
8. **Loading page vs state sheet disagree** (skeleton block vs disabled button). Draft and Approved badges share a gray dot. **Attribution:** the builder ignored it: `skills/design-product-ui/SKILL.md` line 116 (expert move 13, "The skeleton is the layout") together with the sheet's own label. `skills/design-color/references/ui-tokens-themes.md` (status roles) defines `--color-success`, which the Approved badge does not use.
9. **Secondary button border on dark amber tint is 3.04:1**, a 0.04 margin. **Attribution:** silent. FOUNDATIONS `text-muted-on-tint` covers text on tints, but no rule names *borders* on tints. Add "control borders on any `*-subtle` fill use `border-strong` one step up in dark" to `skills/design-color/references/ui-tokens-themes.md`.

### Expert-level gaps
10. **The commit action and the amount never travel together.** Approve is a 107x32 static button. On a 3,219px page (5,156px at 390) it is off screen for 70 to 80% of the scroll, and the dialog's confirm button reads "Approve run", not the amount. Exemplar: Gusto, Rippling and Stripe payouts keep a sticky footer with the total and the commit (opinion, not in the teardowns). The KB's own checkout rule is "The summary never leaves". **Attribution:** for the button amount, the skill says it and the builder ignored it: `skills/design-copy/references/02-ux-microcopy.md` line 27 ("Money buttons show the amount: 'Pay $48.00'"). For the sticky bar, the skill is **silent in the dashboard route**: the rule lives in `skills/design-conversion/references/04-checkout-and-forms.md` line 57, which `design-playbook-dashboard/SKILL.md` line 21 loads only for upgrades. Route it for any money-commit screen.
11. **The approval dialog hides two of the three warnings.** It mentions only Lucía's held pay. The rate alert and the proration are not acknowledged, so a finance lead can approve a 2.6%-above-average BRL trade without seeing it again. **Attribution:** silent. `skills/design-ux/references/04-states-feedback.md` line 64 says only "Friction scales with stakes". Missing rule in `skills/design-product-ui/references/05-states-bulk.md`: "a commit dialog restates every open warning with its effect on the amount, or requires each to be resolved or acknowledged".
12. **Rate lock has no clock.** "Rates are locked for 30 minutes" after approval gives no end time and no countdown. Yet the error state shows the domain knowledge ("ended at 11:22 am ET"). **Attribution:** the skill says it, the builder ignored it: `skills/design-copy/references/02-ux-microcopy.md` line 28 (absolute times for money and deadlines). It is **silent** on live countdowns for time-boxed commitments in `skills/design-motion/references/07-motion-on-real-pages.md`.
13. **FX direction copy.** "BRL is 2.6% above its 30-day average" sits next to 5.4760, a number *below* 5.6200, because rates are quoted per USD. Say what the reader pays: "BRL costs 2.6% more than its 30-day average". **Attribution:** silent. `skills/design-copy/references/06-copy-in-context.md` has no rule that a comparison word must agree with the direction of the number shown beside it.
14. **Status painted on rows.** Three people rows and the panel are both amber. Exemplar: Linear "Borrow": "Status color on a small glyph ... never on the row". **Attribution:** the skill is **contradictory**. `skills/design-layout/references/06-pacing-signature-and-phone-editing.md` line 73 endorses tinted full-bleed alert rows (right inside an alert panel), and `skills/design-product-ui/references/01-tables.md` is silent on tinting data rows. Add: "tint alert rows inside the alert panel; in data tables mark status with glyph plus word only".
15. **Error-state realism (opinion, domain knowledge).** "Return code R01 ... at 11:06 am ET", 14 minutes after a 10:52 approval. ACH R01 returns normally arrive from the receiving bank after settlement, typically the next banking day, not within minutes. A finance lead will notice. Either use a pre-debit balance check ("Chase reported insufficient funds") or move the timeline to the next morning. **Attribution:** silent. No skill asks for a domain plausibility pass on sample states (`skills/design-copy/references/05-content-design-process.md`).
16. **No keyboard layer and only a 2-level breadcrumb.** The builder already names the missing keyboard layer. Linear ships `j/k`, a command menu and shortcuts. The breadcrumb has only 2 levels where the skill says 3 or more. **Attribution:** the builder ignored it: `skills/design-playbook-dashboard/references/signature-moves.md` line 17 (keyboard first) and `skills/design-product-ui/SKILL.md` line 86 (breadcrumbs at 3 or more levels).

## 5. Would Linear's or Stripe's team ship this?

No, but it is closer than most first rounds. The arithmetic is exact to the cent across 38 people and 7 currencies. The September decomposition, the rate-alert sentence and the error timeline are the kind of copy Stripe writes, and the token discipline is tighter than any exemplar measured. What stops a ship is that the screen goes false at its own climax. After Approve, it still calls the rates estimates and still asks for review. In error, it says 38 people paid right under "Nobody has been paid". A Stripe reviewer would also stop at the 1.08:1 selected filter, and would ask why the $265,195.92 being committed is the same size as "7 currencies" and is not on the button that commits it. Linear would cut the row height to 40, drop the doubled amber, and keep the action reachable. Fix the two gate items, then items 3 to 7, and this is a close-to-exemplar screen. The craft is there, but the state model is not finished.
