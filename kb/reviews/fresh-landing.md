## 3. Ranked shortfalls (fundamentals first)

1. **Dark check icons at 2.95:1** (gate 3). Measured on the dark Free and Plus cards. Exemplar: Linear keeps every icon on its surface steps above 3:1 in dark (`linear-home.md`, "Text colors"). Skill: **said, builder ignored**: FOUNDATIONS row 3 names icons "against every surface they sit on in both themes"; the builder's hand-over line checked "dark control borders" only. Tool gap: no lint measures icon ink. Fix: icons use `--color-accent-text` (magenta-300 in dark), or a `--color-icon-accent` role.
2. **Map labels overprint; boundary lines strike labels** (gate 13). "Chichester Harbour" over "Portsmouth" at 1440, 1024, 768, both themes. Exemplar: Stripe's and Linear's figures never overlap text. Skill: **silent**: `skills/design-graphic/references/04-illustration.md` and `skills/design-art-direction/references/06-product-fragments-and-visual-plans.md` have no rule for map or diagram labels; lint `run-together` only compares neighbouring text nodes with less than one em of overlap, so a 96px overprint passes.
3. **The coverage map contradicts the coverage list** (gate 16). A covered Poole station sits in the "Not covered" hatch at every width. Skill: **silent**: no rule that a diagram's geometry is a claim to tick against the list it illustrates. The builder's operate script re-added 14 stations but not where they fall.
4. **Unnamed comparison basis and a mislabelled chart** (gate 16). "Seven days ahead, not three" with no Free-card line; the 16:16 tick sits on the 17:56 label. Skill: **said, builder ignored** for the first (FOUNDATIONS row 16; `claims_inventory.mjs` PERIOD does not catch "X, not Y"); **silent** for the second (no direct-labelling rule for chart markers).
5. **Imagery is all one component, small, and placeless.** Median cover 29% vs Stripe 63%; 5 of 8 fragments are the timeline; 0 photos where both consumer comps lead with pictures. Skill: **partly silent**: `design-art-direction` 06 counts fragments (three or more) but not their variety; the photo route (an honest slot plus a request, SKILL.md "A real photo needed, none available") was followed only as far as the request.
6. **Motion has one scene and a templated heading rise.** Text staggers 594 to 707ms; word rise on 8 headings. Skill: **tool wrong**: `skills/design-motion/assets/motion-plan-check.mjs` lines 256 to 257 only warn between 500 and 800ms ("fine for cards") even when the group is masked word spans it has already counted as text, then prints PASS. **Silent** on reserving the word rise for one heading per page and on a second scene for a product page.
7. **Type out of the measured range.** h1 104px and 61px at 390, three families. Skill: `design-typography` reference 07 allows the display ramp to 112px (FOUNDATIONS "Display ramp"), so the size is **allowed**; opinion: at 390 the clamp minimum should drop to 49px so the product enters the first screen.
8. **State sheet and pause label.** Sheet face "Menu Close"; paused button "play icon + Pause animation". Skill: `skills/design-polish/references/01-interactive-states.md` covers faces, **silent** on toggle labels that must agree with their icon.
9. **Craft details.** Hatch pattern seams where cut segments meet (16:10 on the back lane, 05:40 on the out lane at 768 and 1024); dark echo fill muddy; inshore area name reversed. Skill: silent on lane-anchored patterns (opinion: one `patternUnits="userSpaceOnUse"` pattern per lane).

## 5. Would Stripe's or Linear's team ship this?

Not yet, but this is the first page in the series they would argue about rather than dismiss. The concept is a real one: the day as one strip, chart magenta reserved for the window, and a hero scene that sweeps the day and cuts away the hours that fail, so the motion is the explanation, the way Stripe's checkout scenes are. The data underneath is better than either exemplar's mock-ups: every window is derived from the tide turn, the wind-against-tide start and sunset, and every figure foots. What stops it is the same thing that stopped Ferry, one level up: a defect you only see by looking closely at a picture. Two place names print on top of each other on the coverage map, the map puts Poole in the "Not covered" zone on a page that promises honesty about coverage, a chart tick sits on the wrong label, and the dark pricing icons fall under 3:1. Above the gate, Stripe would want the product bigger and more varied (it is small, and five of the eight fragments are the same strip), and Linear would cut the word rise from seven of the eight headings. Fix the four gate defects and this is a "close" page across the board. Reaching the exemplar needs a second scene, a picture of the sea, and fragments that differ in kind, not only in crop.
