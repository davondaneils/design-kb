## 4. Ranked shortfalls and the skill that should have prevented each

Fundamentals first.

1. **Time without its zone in the hero card at 390 to 1023 (gate 16).** Measured: 5 of 7 rows print a bare local time ("lands 6:48 pm") next to rows that say "ET"; place and rate are cut. Exemplar: Stripe's mock-ups keep full data and scale it down; Linear's product shot shows every field. **Skill:** `skills/design-layout/SKILL.md` gate rule 18 ("no unique fact is cut (collapse or defer instead)") already says it: **builder ignored it**. Also **skill silent**: neither `kb/FOUNDATIONS.md` Content mechanics nor `skills/design-copy/SKILL.md` (line 77, absolute dates) says "when a page shows times in more than one zone, every time carries its zone or place", and `claims_inventory.mjs` cannot flag a zoned time beside an unzoned one. Add both (the rule to FOUNDATIONS Content mechanics via the editor-in-chief, a `ZONE` flag to the inventory).
2. **Count broken from its noun at 390 (gate 16).** "12 / people". **Skill silent**: FOUNDATIONS Content mechanics lists date, time, price, range and number-with-unit, not number-with-count-noun ("12 people", "7 currencies"). Extend the no-break list.
3. **Pressed state reads as disabled (gate 15).** Measured delta E 6.5. Exemplar: Linear and Stripe press darker or press by shadow and scale, never toward the disabled tint. **Skill:** `skills/design-color/SKILL.md` line 82 gives disabled as "neutral-100 fill, neutral-400 text": **builder ignored it** (used `opacity: 0.5` on the brand fill). The skill is also **silent on dark-ink fills**: line 82, `skills/design-polish/references/01-interactive-states.md` line 31 and `skills/design-color/references/ui-tokens-themes.md` line 75 all assume white labels ("600 to 700 hover, 800 pressed"). With dark ink on a mid-orange the builder had to invent a direction and went lighter twice. Add: "on a fill with dark ink, hover and pressed may go lighter or darker, but pressed moves away from the disabled face; check rest, pressed and disabled side by side, delta E 15 or more".
4. **Menu panel text edge misaligned with the logo (gate 13).** 12px offset at 390 and 768. **Skill silent**: `skills/design-layout/SKILL.md` rule 19 ("count the left edges") is written for page sections; `skills/design-ux/references/02-navigation-ia.md` gives no geometry for an opened menu; the `grid-edge` lint only compares sibling columns. Add to design-layout: "padded hit areas in a list hang their padding outside the text edge (negative margin), so the text lines up with the logo and the page grid".
5. **Inline link given a 44px box breaks leading (gate 13).** **Skill said it, builder ignored it**: `skills/design-foundations/references/accessibility.md` line 89 ("Inline links in a sentence are exempt from 2.5.8"). The trap is that one class (`.more`) serves a stand-alone link and an inline one. Add to design-polish: never put `min-block-size` on an element that can sit inside a paragraph; scope touch padding to stand-alone links.
6. **Bulk reading text at 14px.** Scorecard body 14 (Stripe 16, Linear 15). Facts, ledger rows, stat captions, euro note, timeline labels. **Skill said it, builder ignored it**: `skills/design-typography/SKILL.md` F2 ("Web 16px minimum"). 14 is defensible for captions; it is not defensible as the most common size on a marketing page.
7. **Phone measure 33 cpl.** Exemplars 34 to 50 (Stripe 40, Linear 50). Cause: the facts row stays two columns at 390 with 14px text. **Skill silent**: `fundamentals-checklist.md` item 5 and FOUNDATIONS set only a ceiling at 390 ("at most 75"). Add a phone floor (about 35 characters) to design-typography reference 01 and propose it for the lint.
8. **Contrast margin of 0.02 on the dark card's control borders** (3.02:1). **Skill partly says it**: FOUNDATIONS `text-muted-on-tint` rule asks to measure every muted-text and tint pair in both themes, but there is no matching `border-strong-on-raised` rule for non-text. Add to design-color ui-tokens-themes: measure border-strong on every raised surface in dark; keep 3.3:1 or more for margin.
9. **Phone nav hides every link and the primary action.** At 390 the header is logo plus "Menu"; the opened menu has no "Start free trial", and the trigger never changes to "Close". **Skill said it, builder ignored it**: `skills/design-ux/SKILL.md` line 58 ("On phones keep the two or three most used links visible plus a labelled Menu"). Disclosure state of the trigger: **skill silent** in design-ux reference 02.
10. **Hanging punctuation missed on the pull quote.** The opening quote mark sits on the text edge (1440 tile 03, x 80), so "We" is indented against the label above. **Skill said it, builder ignored it**: `skills/design-layout/SKILL.md` expert move 11 and `references/01-grids-and-structure.md` line 79.
11. **No ownable image above the fold.** The concept ("every picture is a crossing") is good and written down, but the hero shows a table. Exemplar: Stripe's one gradient carried into every card; Linear's full-width product. **Skill says the placement** (`skills/design-art-direction/SKILL.md` step 5: "hero, at least one mid-page section, and one small surface") but **is silent on the bar**: nothing says the motif must be visible in the first screen as a drawn thing, not only as a word. Add a reject test to the playbook: "hide the logo; can you still tell whose page it is from the first screen?" (`skills/design-playbook-landing/references/signature-moves.md`).
12. **Motion is a token exercise, not a voice.** 1 duration and 1 curve measured, against 5 to 8 and 4 to 9. **Skills contradict each other** (builder's skill problem 3, confirmed): `skills/design-motion/references/03-choreography-transitions.md` line 49 ("Hero: no entrance animation") against `kb/FOUNDATIONS.md` Motion ("one hero reveal per page up to 800ms") and `skills/design-playbook-landing/SKILL.md` line 19. A builder reading both chose none. Also **silent** on animating native `<details>` (`::details-content` plus `interpolate-size`), so the FAQ snaps.
13. **Proof late and all sample.** Fold carries four product facts; evidence (quote, stats) at roughly 52% of the page. Exemplar: Stripe's live counter and logo strip at the fold. **Skill partly says it**: `skills/design-playbook-landing/SKILL.md` line 33 ("Proof sits next to the claim it proves"), which the hero card arguably satisfies. design-conversion `references/02-proof-and-trust.md` should say where proof sits on a page whose proof must be labelled sample.
14. **Tablet is a stretched phone.** 768: one column, right third empty through steps and FAQ. **Skill thin**: `skills/design-layout/references/04-responsive-and-adaptive.md` line 17 gives the 640 to 1023 band one line ("1, or list-detail at 768 and up if content is dense") and no marketing-page tablet pattern; the teardowns have no 768 rows to compare against.
15. **Dark theme is a remap.** Solid hex hairlines, same-chroma orange band. Exemplar: Linear borders `rgba(255,255,255,.05 to .12)`, surfaces 1 to 3 lightness points apart. **Skill:** `skills/design-color/references/ui-tokens-themes.md` covers mapping roles but not lowering chroma of large brand fields in dark (opinion).
16. **Copy polish.** Four wordings of the same-day promise; "per person ... for each person"; "teams of 5 to 200" against "From 1 to 200 people". **Skill said it**: FOUNDATIONS Content mechanics "each promise has one wording"; `claims_inventory.mjs` did not raise PROMISE, so the tool missed it too (the wordings differ by more than its heuristic).
17. **Small state craft.** Theme-switch focus ring overlapping the selected pill; FAQ hover dims the question to muted (lower contrast on hover); 1.5px icons beside 20px/500 text (lint warns 8 times). **Skills:** design-polish `references/01-interactive-states.md` (hover should add, not remove, emphasis) and `references/03-shape-alignment-icons.md` (stroke matched to text weight): said, builder accepted the warnings.

## 5. Would Stripe's or Linear's team ship this?

No, but it is closer than most first rounds, and the gap is not where the tools look. The engineering is honest: every number reconciles to the cent and the minute, time zones are right across a DST boundary, contrast clears with margin, and the serif-plus-Inter voice with the italic turn is a real point of view. A Stripe reviewer would stop at the hero card on a laptop at 1024, read "lands 6:48 pm" under "Rates locked 8:52 am ET", and ask whose 6:48. That is the kind of truth slip their mock-ups never make, because they scale data rather than cut it. A Linear reviewer would press the button and see it fade toward disabled, open the phone menu and find its text 12px off the logo, and feel that the page never moves. Above the gate, the page is competent and restrained where the exemplars are memorable: no ownable image, proof that arrives late, a tablet layout that is a stretched phone, and dark mode that remaps tokens instead of retuning them. Fix the three gate items (all small) and it reaches "close". Reaching the exemplars needs a drawn crossing motif in the first screen and a motion voice.
