## 3. Ranked shortfalls (fundamentals first, then expert gaps)

1. **Stepper buttons do nothing; the wrong one disables at 200** (gate 15, 16). Measured: value stays 12, total stays $108 by mouse, keyboard and touch; at 200 "One fewer person" disables. Exemplar: Ramp turns a section into a working tool (the company-URL box, `ramp-home.md`, "Copy and UX"); a calculator that does not calculate is worse than none. Skill: **silent**. `skills/design-director/SKILL.md` line 90 asks to click every *primary* action, and line 71 plus `skills/design-polish/references/01-interactive-states.md` line 77 put states on a state sheet whose faces are pinned with classes, which hides dead handlers. Needed rule: operate every control on the live page (mouse, Enter, Space, tap) and assert the output changed, and scope selectors to the component (`calc.querySelectorAll`, not `document.querySelectorAll` on a generic data attribute).
2. **Hero total does not foot; "0.4% shown on every line" is not shown** (gate 16). Measured: $74,537.54 vs $74,536.85 from the displayed rates; no line shows a fee. BUILD_NOTES also claims "every row cost is shown to 2 decimals" and "9:14 am ET, in the card at all widths"; neither is on the renders. Exemplar: Stripe's mock-ups show subtotal, tax and total that add up ("$150.00", `stripe-home.md`, "Imagery"). Skill: **says it, builder ignored**: `skills/design-copy/references/06-copy-in-context.md` line 52 ("Reconcile, do not proofread"). The builder reconciled against `src/numbers.mjs` (unrounded rates), not against the page. Skill gap: add "a total must foot from the figures as displayed, at their displayed precision; if rates are rounded, show a per-line dollar cost or derive the total from the rounded rates".
3. **Open FAQ item looks closed under reduced motion** (gate 15). Measured: "+" on the open row in `ferry-state-faq-1440-light.png` and on the state sheet. Exemplar: Mercury animates accordion rows with `grid-template-rows .3s` and keeps the open state (`mercury-home.md`, "Motion"). Skill: **says it, but one line invites the bug**. `skills/design-motion/SKILL.md` line 24 says "meaning survives"; `skills/design-motion/references/04-micro-interactions.md` line 20 says "chevron rotates in the same time; off under reduced motion", which reads as "rotation off". Fix the line to: "under reduce, the icon snaps to its open state (rotate with no transition, or swap plus for minus); only the transition is removed".
4. **Unique facts cut at 390 and 1024.** Measured: the 390 card drops People, Locked rate, country names and the step dates; 1024 drops Locked rate. Exemplar: Linear keeps the product shot's content and crops it (`linear-home.md`, "Layout"). Skill: **says it, builder ignored**: `skills/design-layout/references/06-pacing-signature-and-phone-editing.md` line 12 ("No unique content is cut at any width") and line 76 ("Never: cut a unique fact").
5. **Outlined footer wordmark shows overlapping contours.** Measured: stray crossbar contours through "f" and "e" at 1440 and 390, both themes. Exemplar: none of the four ships a stroked glyph outline; Stripe and Linear use filled marks. Skill: **silent**. `skills/design-graphic/SKILL.md` line 96 and `references/01-logo-identity.md` line 93 recommend the outlined sign-off, and `skills/design-graphic/assets/wordmark.py` does not remove overlaps. Needed: "variable fonts keep overlapping contours; remove overlaps (fontTools `removeOverlaps` or a boolean union) before any stroke-only rendering, and check the stroked render at 400% zoom".
6. **Dark header takes a pink cast.** Measured: header `rgb(20,15,18)` over page `rgb(14,18,23)`; computed value `oklch(0.180 0.012 none / 0.88)`, the hue lost when mixing with `transparent` in oklch, plus `saturate(1.4)` on the backdrop. Exemplar: Linear sets `theme-color` and header to the page `#08090a` exactly (`linear-home.md`, live capture). Skill: **wrong**: `skills/design-polish/references/02-depth-borders-shadows.md` line 102 prescribes `color-mix(in oklch, var(--color-bg) 85%, transparent)`. Change to `oklch(from var(--color-bg) l c h / 0.85)` or mix in srgb, and add a pixel check of header vs page in dark.
7. **Copy leaves the finance lead's decisive questions unanswered.** Missing: same-day cutoff time and zone, who holds the balance (bank partner, licensing, safeguarding), tax and contractor forms; plus "From 1 to 200" vs "teams of 5 to 200". Exemplar: Mercury states its legal status in the hero ("Mercury is a fintech company, not an FDIC-insured bank", `mercury-home.md`, "Hierarchy"). Skill: **partly silent**: `skills/design-conversion/references/02-proof-and-trust.md` lines 26 and 37 cover SOC 2 for B2B security teams but say nothing about where customer money sits for money-movement products; BUILD_NOTES names the gap but left it out rather than writing a labelled sample.
8. **Product shown once, static.** Measured: one card, one load reveal; "Paying, 4 of 7" never progresses. Exemplar: Stripe shows checkout, billing and connect mock-ups; Linear shows a multi-pane app (`stripe-home.md`, `linear-home.md`, "Imagery"). Skill: **says it, builder ignored**: `skills/design-playbook-landing/references/signature-moves.md` line 27 ("Motion that explains... a state change") and line 18 ("The product is the hero image").
9. **Composition voids and wandering column starts at 1440.** Measured: rate band and final call leave the right 55% of the container empty; right-hand content starts at x 523, 627, 732, 837 and 941 across sections. Exemplar: Stripe's visible 1266px grid lines hold every card to the same edges (`stripe-home.md`, "Layout and spacing"). Skill: **partly wrong**: `signature-moves.md` line 23 asks for "identical" section padding, which the builder followed into monotony; nothing asks for a fixed set of two or three column starts per page.
10. **Proof adds no fact.** Measured: the three figures (3, 12, 1) restate the quote's own "12 people in 7 countries" and "three tools"; "1 run, approved once" is filler. Exemplar: Stripe "135+ currencies", "$1.9T processed", "99.999% uptime" (`stripe-home.md`, "Copy and UX"). Skill: `skills/design-conversion/references/02-proof-and-trust.md` line 15 ("specific facts only this company could state") **says it**; the builder ignored it.
11. **Type details below the exemplar bar.** h1 72px over the exemplar 64 ceiling, h2 at 600 heavier than the 500 h1, six orange eyebrows. Exemplar: Stripe and Ramp hold one weight; Stripe, Linear and Ramp use same-size two-tone headings (`INDEX.md` section 3). Skill: **silent in the skill**, present only in `kb/teardowns/INDEX.md` section 3; `skills/design-typography` has no two-tone heading rule.
12. **Font fallback shifts layout by 161px at 390** (lint warn). Skill: `skills/design-typography/references/06-type-without-web-fonts.md` covers metric fallbacks; BUILD_NOTES lists `size-adjust` values yet the lint still measures the shift, so the override is not taking (opinion: check that the fallback `@font-face` is the one named second in `font-family`).

## 5. Would Stripe's or Linear's team ship this?

No. The page is calm, legible and well-tokened: contrast is better than either exemplar, the 4px grid is spotless, the concept (one balance splitting into lanes) is ownable and drawn consistently, and the copy voice is close to Stripe's plainness. But the only working tool on the page, the pricing stepper, does nothing when pressed, the hero's headline number does not add up from the figures beside it, the rate section promises a fee "shown on every line" that is not on any line, and reduced motion users cannot tell an open answer from a closed one. Those are the exact defects a payments company cannot ship, because its whole pitch is that the numbers are exact. Above the gate, the product is shown once and statically where Stripe and Linear show depth, the footer wordmark exposes broken glyph contours, and the proof restates the quote instead of adding a fact. Fix the four gate defects and this is a "close" page; reaching the exemplar needs a second product moment, a state change that moves, and the trust facts a finance lead needs before moving payroll.
