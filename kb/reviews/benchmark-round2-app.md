## 3. Ranked shortfalls

Fundamentals first, then expert gaps. "Said and ignored" means a skill line already covers it; "silent" means no skill line does; "wrong" means the skill line produces the defect.

### Fundamentals (gate)

1. **A false universal claim in the commit dialog and the default screen (gate 16).**
   - Measured: dialog body "Everyone is paid on Fri, Oct 30." then the list item "Tomás Herrera has no bank details. MX$64,500.00 ($3,503.15) is held in your Ferry balance, not sent on Fri, Oct 30." Default steps card "Everyone is paid Fri, Oct 30. In their own currency, to their own bank." Default answer "38 people in 7 currencies get paid on Fri, Oct 30." The approved state already says "37 people get paid", so the builder knew.
   - Exemplar: Linear states state "in words next to the icon"; Stripe's "critical: Should be used sparingly" role language (`stripe-dashboard.md` "Copy and UX"). Neither would let a universal sit beside its exception.
   - Skill: `skills/design-copy/references/06-copy-in-context.md` line 7 ("Every number is a claim, and it is true") is said, but nothing names quantifiers ("everyone", "all", "nobody") as claims, and `assets/claims_inventory.mjs` lists numbers, not quantifiers: **silent**. `skills/design-playbook-dashboard/SKILL.md` line 42 asks the dialog to restate every warning with its effect, which the builder did, and is **silent** on the body sentence above the list. Fix: a rule "a universal claim never shares a screen with its exception: write 37 of 38, or say who is excluded" in design-copy 06, and a `QUANTIFIER` warning in `claims_inventory.mjs` when "everyone, all, every, nobody, none" appears on a page that also renders an alert or a held, excluded or failed item.

2. **Focus hidden behind the open phone drawer (gate 1, WCAG 2.4.11).**
   - Measured: at 390 with the drawer open, the 8th Tab lands on "Ask Tomás for details" (60,410 to 230,458); all three probe points hit `#sidebar`. `main` is not inert; no `aria-modal`.
   - Exemplar: Stripe and Vercel drawers are modal; any APG modal pattern contains focus.
   - Skill: `skills/design-ux/SKILL.md` line 30 ("never hidden behind sticky bars or drawers (WCAG 2.4.11)"), `skills/design-frontend/references/01-semantic-html.md` line 21 (`inert` behind a drawer) and `skills/design-product-ui/references/04-shells-density.md` line 29 ("opens the sidebar as a modal drawer"): **said and ignored**. `tools/lint.mjs` has no check that opens `?open=menu` and tabs: **silent**. Fix: build the drawer as `<dialog>` with `showModal()` (or set `inert` on everything else); add a lint pass that, when an `aria-expanded` control opens a fixed panel, tabs 20 times and fails any focused element whose center is covered.

3. **The rate lock grows after Retry (gate 16, true after every primary action).**
   - Measured: `?state=error` shows "24 min left" (11:22 minus 10:58). Retry navigates to `?state=approved`, which shows "29 min left". The approved state is the post-Approve screen, reused as the post-Retry screen.
   - Skill: `skills/design-playbook-dashboard/SKILL.md` line 47 ("a post-action state for every primary action"): **said and ignored**; Retry needed its own `?state=retrying`. `skills/design-product-ui/references/05-states-bulk.md` line 44 says a countdown "counts down in place": **said**. `tools/lint-truth.mjs` checks `data-monotonic` within one page, not across a state transition: **silent**. Fix: in the playbook, "a primary action that is not the first one (Retry, Resubmit) gets its own post-action URL; never reuse the first action's"; in the lint, compare `data-countdown` before and after each `[data-retry]`, `[data-open-confirm]` navigation and fail on an increase.

4. **One label, two numbers after the filter (gate 16).**
   - Measured: after "Needs a look 3", the section reads "People in this run 3"; the summary card's identical label reads "People in this run 38".
   - Exemplar: Vercel "All (1,772)" count pills (`vercel-dashboard.md` line 47) put the count on the filter, not the heading.
   - Skill: `skills/design-copy/references/02-ux-microcopy.md` line 28 ("KPI and column labels are true in every state") covers KPIs, **silent** on filtered views. Fix: add "a filtered view reads 'Showing 3 of 38'; the heading's count never changes meaning" to that line and to `design-product-ui/references/01-tables.md`.

5. **Broken sum rule and a stray separator (gate 13).**
   - Measured: currency table total rule starts at x=532, not x=272 (`tfoot th` has no top border). At 390 in approved and error the BRL row ends with a lone "·" line.
   - Exemplar: Stripe draws keylines as rings so edges never break (`stripe-dashboard.md` "Layout and spacing").
   - Skill: `skills/design-product-ui/references/01-tables.md` has no total-row rule: **silent**. `skills/design-product-ui/SKILL.md` line 67 describes the two-line phone item and is **silent** on how line 2 is joined. Fix: "the total row's rule spans the full row (style `tfoot > tr > *`, not `td`)"; "build line 2 from items joined by CSS (`span + span::before { content: ' · ' }`) so a hidden item takes its separator with it". The `grid-edge` lint could also measure border continuity across a row's cells.

6. **State fidelity (gate 15).**
   - Measured: state sheet ghost "pressed" renders identical to "rest" (no `.btn.ghost.is-pressed` rule). Loading state shows the full-strength primary with a spinner, which reads as "approving now". The skeleton has no answer line and no Needs-a-look block.
   - Skill: `skills/design-polish/references/06-polish-checklist.md` line 24 (every state differs) and `skills/design-product-ui/references/05-states-bulk.md` line 67 ("The skeleton is the layout. Same card, same header, same row count"): **said and ignored**. `skills/design-polish/references/01-interactive-states.md` line 17 defines busy for after a submit and is **silent** on the commit button during page load. `tools/lint.mjs` `state-sheet` does not compare cells: **silent**. Fix: lint fails two differently captioned cells in one sheet row whose pixels match; the polish chapter adds "while the page loads, the commit button is disabled (neutral fill, label kept), never busy: busy means the person's own action is in flight".

### Expert gaps (above the gate)

7. **Status color reuses the brand.** Info, the "now" step, Locked and Funding are `#006ebe`, the same value as `--color-brand` and the Approve fill, so "Partial month" reads as a link-colored call to act. `skills/design-playbook-dashboard/SKILL.md` line 32 ("Status colors ... never reuse the brand color"): **said and ignored**. Linear keeps blue-violet `#5e6ad2` for Done and never for actions (`linear-app.md` "Status colors").

8. **The exceptions are not where the decision is.** Default sort is USD descending, so the three flagged people sit at rows 26, 35 and 38 of 38; 35 rows carry a green "Ready" check; at 390 the review block starts below the first screen; none of the three alerts can be resolved in place (no "Hold Lucas's payment", "Exclude from this run", or "Edit proration"; the only path is "Approving accepts all three as they are"). Linear's default is quiet: status hue only where state differs (`linear-app.md` "Color"). Skill: `skills/design-product-ui/references/01-tables.md` line 35 and `SKILL.md` line 69 ("glyph plus word" per row) were followed literally and are **silent** on suppressing the default status; `skills/design-product-ui/references/06-dashboards.md` line 26 moves action alerts up for analytical pages and is **silent** for review-and-commit at 390. Fix (opinion): on review-and-commit tables, the default status is a muted word with no glyph or no cell text at all, flagged rows pin to the top under the default sort, and every alert offers one in-place resolution beside its information link.

9. **Density of the first screen.** Alerts are 84px each where `skills/design-layout/references/06-pacing-signature-and-phone-editing.md` lines 52 and 73 measured about 55px, "one line each": **said and ignored** (the detail is capped at 52ch inside a 1,200px row, so it wraps). Nav rows 32 against Linear 28; page head 64 against Linear 44. Result: zero people rows in the first 900px at 1440.

10. **Decisions a finance lead faces that the screen does not answer.** No approve-by time for the pay date (an ACH debit must settle before Fri, Oct 30: "Approve by Tue, Oct 27, 5 pm ET to pay on Fri, Oct 30"); no quote age or refresh while the estimate sits on screen ("quoted at 10:48 am ET", but how old is it now?); no statement of what happens if the lock expires before a retry succeeds (new quote, new total). Skill: `skills/design-product-ui/references/06-dashboards.md` line 28 (review-and-commit) is **silent** on cut-off times and quote staleness. Fix: add both to the review-and-commit rule; Stripe pairs every figure with its period (`design-playbook-dashboard/SKILL.md` line 40), and a cut-off is the period of a commit.

11. **Dark theme collapses the sunken well.** Dark `surface-sunken` and `bg` are both neutral-990 (#101214): the workspace block vanishes from the sidebar (1.00:1) and the badges and segmented track nearly vanish on `surface` (1.08:1). `kb/FOUNDATIONS.md` line 23 ("one step darker than `surface` in dark") produces exactly this when `bg` is already that step: **wrong**. `skills/design-color/references/ui-tokens-themes.md` lines 16 to 36 has no `surface-sunken` row: **silent**. Fix for the editor-in-chief: in dark, a well that sits on `bg` uses a lighter step or an alpha fill (`rgb(255 255 255 / 0.04)`, the Linear selection wash), and the role table gets a sunken row with a measured delta against both `bg` and `surface`.

12. **The error state says the same fact five times.** Header badge "Funding failed", alert title "the bank rejected the funding transfer ... at 10:58 am ET", answer line "Chase rejected the funding transfer at 10:58 am ET", summary "The transfer ... was rejected", step "Funding failed at 10:58 am ET"; the lock end 11:22 appears three times. `skills/design-copy/references/06-copy-in-context.md` line 25 ("each says something new"): **said and ignored**. The answer line should carry only the consequence and the deadline ("Nobody has been paid. Retry before 11:22 am ET to keep this total.").

13. **The rounding basis is named only on phones.** "Each payment converted, then rounded to the cent" is in the 390 footer only; at 768 and up a finance lead who multiplies CA$57,200.00 by 1/1.3712 gets $41,715.29, not the shown $41,715.28 (also GBP and EUR, 1 cent each). `skills/design-playbook-dashboard/SKILL.md` line 40 ("One comparison basis, named where it is read"): **said and ignored** at desktop widths.

14. **Sort is invisible until used.** The Person header's arrow is `opacity: 0` at rest and on hover (measured 0), so the two sortable headers look like the four that are not; phones have no sort at all. `skills/design-product-ui/references/01-tables.md` line 83 warns against "Sort arrows on every column with no state" and is **silent** on how a sortable but unsorted column is shown. Fix (opinion): reveal a muted arrow on header hover and focus; a phone list gets a "Sort: USD, high to low" control.

15. **Foundation conformance, minor.** h1, dialog h2 and empty h2 at 20/28 (1.4) against FOUNDATIONS headings 1.1 to 1.25 (`kb/FOUNDATIONS.md` line 14); light `--elevation-popover` and `--elevation-modal` include `--highlight`, where FOUNDATIONS' light recipe is ring plus shadow only (line 48 to 49; harmless on white, but not the contract). Every other token name and value checked (space 0 to 128, text 12 to 39, radius 0 to 16 and full, durations 100 to 400, the three easings, `--motion-*`, shadows 0 to 4, `--state-*` at 8, 10, 10, 16 percent, icon 12 to 24 at 1.5px, control 32, 40, 48) matches v0.4.5.

## 4. Overall

Would Linear's or Stripe's team ship this? No, and the reasons are not taste. The screen's numbers are perfect: every cent reconciles in every state, the error copy (R29, the ACH company ID, positive pay) is better than most real payroll products, and the contrast beats every exemplar. But the one dialog that moves $313,282.88 tells the finance lead "Everyone is paid on Fri, Oct 30" two lines above the person who is not; the Retry path makes a rate lock grow by five minutes; the phone drawer lets focus disappear under itself; and the sum rule of the money table is broken under its own label. Those are the gate. Above it, the screen is a careful, competent build that reads as one notch airier and louder than Linear: status hue spent 35 times on "Ready", the info color borrowed from the brand, three tinted bands that outshout the commit, the three people who need a decision buried at rows 26, 35 and 38, and no answer to the question a payroll lead asks first, which is "by when must I approve?". Fix the four gate items and the screen is close; to reach Linear it needs to be quieter by default and to put every exception, with a way to resolve it, where the eye lands first.
