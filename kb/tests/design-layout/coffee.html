How we roast

We roast on a 1962 Probat in Southeast Portland, twelve kilos at a time. Every batch is logged, and we print what we paid for every lot on the bag.

  1. 1

    Buy direct

    We pay growers we know by name, and we visit every farm we buy from at least once every two years.

  2. 2

    Roast in small batches

    Twelve kilos at a time, profiled for each lot and cupped by two of us before a single bag ships.

  3. 3

    Ship within 48 hours

    Your coffee leaves the roastery two days after roasting at most, so it peaks in your kitchen, not ours.
