What happens in your 90 minutes

Most people wait years before they book a hearing test. So when you do come in, we don’t hurry. This is what the time is for.

Start90 minutes later

  1. We listen

    You tell us what you’ve noticed, where it’s hardest, and what you want to be able to hear again.

  2. We test

    We look in your ears, then test your hearing in our soundproof booth. Nothing hurts.

  3. We explain

    We show you your results on paper, in plain English, and answer every question you have.

  4. You decide, later

    If aids would help, we talk through the options. Then you go home. There’s nothing to sign.
