# Strategy brief: Halden Hearing

Depth: full (new identity, positioning missing; website-strategy stages 2 to 5 in `discovery.md`)
Locale: en-GB (Clifton, Bristol patients)
Deliverables: brief, homepage hero and next section, mark, hand-over
Not built: guidelines, logo kit, booking flow

## 1. Audience moment
Moment: people in their sixties and seventies when family lunches become nodding along, wanting to follow conversation again, held back by fear of being sold a device on the spot.
Primary CEP (hero): "I can hear people, I just can't make out the words"
Other CEPs: a GP referral; aids bought elsewhere in a drawer
Anxiety and design answer: pressure to buy -> "no commission, nothing to buy" beside the button; every price in the next section
Their words: "everyone mumbles", "hearing test Bristol" (source: stand-in, NIA symptom list, brief search term)

## 2. Positioning
Alternatives: status quo: put it off, turn the TV up; a chain's free test; another independent
Competitors named: Specsavers, Boots Hearingcare, Amplifon
Competitor wall: navy or green, rounded sans, smiling stock faces or devices, "Book a free hearing test" (captured 2026-10-13, `wall/`)
Category frame: independent audiologists, hearing test in Clifton (tried: hearing aids, hearing care)
Onlyness (internal): Our clinic is the only hearing test in Clifton given 90 minutes by an audiologist on no commission, for people who have put it off for years.
Swap test: pass; Specsavers, Boots Hearingcare and Amplifon cannot say two named audiologists, 90 minutes, Clifton; independents unverified

## 3. One true fact and proof
Fact: every appointment is 90 minutes (source: Priya Nair and Tom Ashby, brief 2026-10-13)
Proof on the page: the number in the H1; full prices; two names; no commission
Behaviors: no commission on any of four manufacturers; fits and adjusts aids bought elsewhere
Weakest field: proof; no reviews or patient words, and the independents' appointment length is unscanned

## 7. Concept for art direction
Concept sentence: Ninety minutes to find the missing sounds: every image is an everyday sentence set twice, as heard with its quiet s, f and th gone, and as said.
Reject test: Not a real sentence shown as heard and as said? A face, device, ear or sound wave: cut it.
