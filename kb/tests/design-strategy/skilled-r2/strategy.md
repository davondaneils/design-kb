# Strategy brief: Halden Hearing

Depth: one page (no strategy on file; facts given)
Locale: en-GB (Bristol patients)
Deliverables: brief; homepage first screen and next section; hand-over
Not built: booking flow, other pages, logo kit, photos

## 1. Audience moment
Moment: Bristol adults in their 60s and 70s when family or a GP finally pushes them to check hearing they have doubted for years, wanting to know where they stand, held back by fear of a sales pitch.
Primary CEP (hero): "I keep meaning to get my hearing checked" (the situation that brings them)
Anxiety and design answer: a sales pitch -> "no obligation to buy" beside the button; prices below
Their words: "hearing test Bristol" (source: clinic search terms)

## 2. Positioning
Alternatives: status quo: put it off, turn the TV up; a chain test
Competitors named: Specsavers, Boots Hearingcare, Amplifon
Competitor wall: green and blue, stock couples, ear icons (from memory)
Category frame: hearing test in Bristol (tried: hearing aid shop; independent audiologist)
Onlyness (internal): Our clinic is the only Clifton hearing practice whose owners see every patient for 90 minutes, on no commission.
Swap test: pass; false for each named competitor

## 3. One true fact and proof
Fact: every appointment is 90 minutes with Priya Nair or Tom Ashby, owners on no commission (source: founders, 2026)
Proof on the page: the 90 minutes, step by step; 4 in 10 new patients referred
Behaviors: servicing aids bought elsewhere
Weakest field: proof; no reviews yet
