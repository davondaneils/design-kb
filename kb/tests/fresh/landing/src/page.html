How a window is found.|Three tests, every hour of the day.

You set your boat’s limits once. For each hour, Halyard asks whether the stream is with you, whether the wind is inside your limits and whether you will be home before dark. Where all three say yes, you have a window.

Tide with you

Halyard sends you out on a fair stream and back on the next one. On Saturday it runs east from 05:40 to 11:10, then west until 17:50: out in the morning, back after the turn.

Wind inside your limits

Set the most your boat and crew will take. Wren’s limits are 20 kn of wind and 12 kn against the tide. From 15:00 a 15 kn westerly meets the west-going stream, so the afternoon is cut.

Home before dark

Halyard works back from sunset to the latest time you can leave. Sunset at Lymington is 17:56 and the passage takes 1 h 40 min, so Wren leaves Cowes by 16:16.

One plan, to the minute.|Out at 08:30, home by 14:10.

Halyard times each leg at your boat’s speed with the stream added, so you know where you will be at each mark and how much daylight you have left. On Saturday that is 3 h 46 min to spare before sunset.

Questions.|Asked on the pontoon.

Can I navigate with Halyard?{{icon:plus}}

No. Halyard is a planning aid for choosing when to go. It is not a substitute for official tide tables, charts or forecasts, and its maps are illustrations, not charts.

Which boats is it for?{{icon:plus}}

Yachts and dayboats from 6 to 12 metres. You set the length, cruising speed and limits for each boat, and Halyard times every leg at that speed with the stream added.

Where does it work?{{icon:plus}}

From Anvil Point to Selsey Bill: Poole Harbour, the Solent and Chichester Harbour, with 14 tidal height stations and 8 stream points. It does not cover Channel crossings or anywhere west of Anvil Point or east of Selsey Bill.

What does Plus cost, and how do I cancel?{{icon:plus}}

Plus is £4.99 a month or £39 a year. Cancel online in Settings at any time; Plus runs to the end of the period you have paid for, then your account goes back to Free.

Does it work without a signal?{{icon:plus}}

With Plus, your plans and the day’s data are kept on your phone, so they open at sea. New warnings arrive when you are back in signal.
