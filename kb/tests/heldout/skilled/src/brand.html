Set in Stone, brand guidelines

Concept: every image is the litho stone and the sheet it prints. The stone carries the name in reverse; the sheet carries it right-reading in ink and Glasgow red sandstone. Reject test: if an image shows no stone, no print and no act of printing (mirror, grain, registration), cut it.

The wordmark

Bodoni Moda 800 at optical size 28, outlined. One quirk: the S of Stone is drawn mirror-reversed, as it sits on the stone, so on the stone it reads the right way round. Clear space is the cap height of the S on every side; minimum width 96 px on screen, 30 mm in print.

Colour, in proportion

Mostly cream and ink. Sandstone is one moment per surface: the italic in, a price, the primary button. Ratios measured with oklch_scale.py.

Type

Display: Bodoni Moda (OFL, free for web, print and logo use), roman 700 and italic 500, pinned at optical size 48 for 39 px and up. Text and labels: the system sans. Nothing below 40 px on a 1080 social canvas.
