How a run works

Fund once, approve once, everyone is paid.

  1. 01 Fund

    Send dollars to your Ferry balance by ACH or wire. One balance covers every country you pay.

  2. 02 Approve

    Check what each person receives in their currency and what it costs you. The rate locks when you approve.

  3. 03 Everyone paid

    Ferry pays each person in their local currency the same day. You get one receipt for the whole run.

Questions

Before you run payroll with us.

Something else? Book a demo and ask a person.

When exactly does the rate lock?

When you approve the run. From that moment the amount each person receives and your dollar cost are fixed, even if the market moves before the money lands.

How fast do people get paid?

The same day you approve, in all 13 countries. Each person gets the money in their local currency, into their own bank account.

Is Ferry an employer of record?

No. Ferry pays people you already employ or contract. If you need to hire in a country where you have no company, you need an employer of record first; Ferry can still pay your contractors there.

What does it cost?

$12 per person per month, first 3 people free. You pay only for the people you paid that month. Exchange is the mid-market rate plus 0.4%, with no transfer fees.

Can I cancel?

Yes, online any time from Settings. There is no contract and no cancellation fee. Money left in your balance goes back to your bank.
