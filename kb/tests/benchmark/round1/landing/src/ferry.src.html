How a run works

Fund it, approve it, and everyone is paid that day.

  1. Fund

    Add US dollars to your Ferry balance by ACH or wire. One balance pays every country on the run.

  2. Approve

    Check names, amounts and rates, then approve by 9 am ET on a business day. Each rate locks the moment you approve.

  3. Everyone paid

    Each person is paid in their local currency that day. The run shows when each payment lands, in that person’s local time.

Questions

What founders ask before the first run

Read the docs
What does “the same day” mean?

Approve the run by 9 am ET on a business day and each payment lands that day in the person’s own time zone. The run lists every landing time before you approve.

Which exchange rate do my people get?

The mid-market rate at the moment you approve, with no FX markup. The rate locks for that run, and each payslip shows it with the time it was locked.

Does Ferry employ my people or file their taxes?

No. Ferry pays the people you already employ or contract with; it is not an employer of record. Local tax filings stay with your accountant or payroll provider, and Ferry gives them a file of every payment.

How do I fund the balance?

In US dollars, by ACH or wire. A wire arrives the same business day; ACH takes 1 to 3 business days, so fund ahead of payday. The run will not let you approve more than your balance.

What does it cost?

$12 per person a month, first 3 people free. You pay only for people paid that month. No FX markup, no setup fee and no transfer fees.

Where is our money held?

In a segregated account at a US partner bank, kept apart from Ferry’s own money. Your balance is only used for the runs you approve.

Can we leave whenever we want?

Yes. Cancel online at any time, and export every run, rate and payslip as CSV first. Any money left in your balance goes back to your bank account.
