How we roast

We roast on a 1962 Probat in Southeast Portland, 12 kilos at a time. Every batch is logged, and we print what we paid for every lot on the bag.

Between the farm and your door

Bought by name, tasted twice, shipped the day after the roast.

  1. 1

    We know every grower by name

    We buy direct and visit every farm we buy from at least once every two years. The ledger above is what lot 0412 cost.

  2. 2

    We taste every batch twice

    Each batch is cupped the morning after its roast, then again before it is packed. A batch that misses its profile becomes cafe espresso, not your bag.

  3. 3

    It ships the day after the roast

    We roast on Tuesdays and Fridays and ship each batch the day after, on Wednesdays and Saturdays, so the coffee peaks in your kitchen, not on our shelf.
