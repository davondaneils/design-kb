# Strategy brief: Crux member schedule

Depth: inherit (one screen inside the Crux product; the test brief names no brand, positioning or site work)
Locale: en-US (fictional US gym, Northline Climbing, Brooklyn location, zone America/New_York)
Deliverables: schema, access rules, API surface, the member class schedule screen with every state, hand-over
Not built: brand identity, logo system, marketing site, staff desk screens, owner attendance screen (the brief asks for the data and API behind them, not their screens)

Brand source: none on file for Crux; the screen inherits the design-kb FOUNDATIONS tokens (v0.4.4) and one product accent
Must not change: mobile web conventions (Back works, 44px targets, one filled action per view), FOUNDATIONS type and spacing scales, the location time zone as the only clock on screen

## 1. Audience moment
Moment: a member of a climbing gym when they plan the week on their phone, wanting a place in the class they need, held back by full classes and fear of a no-show charge they did not see coming.
Primary CEP (hero): "Can I still get in, and what happens if I cancel?"
Their words: "am I on the waitlist", "can I still cancel", "why can't I book" (source: stand-in, the brief's own rules restated as member questions; no research held)
Anxiety and design answer: a surprise no-show -> every booked row says the free-cancel deadline as an absolute time in the gym's zone, and the late cancel asks for a second, explicit tap
