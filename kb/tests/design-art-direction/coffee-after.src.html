How we roast

Slow, small and on a 1962 Probat.

We roast in Southeast Portland on a drum roaster older than any of us. Every batch is logged; this is the one in your bag.

  1. 01

    Buy direct

    We pay growers we know by name, on average 2.4 times the Fairtrade minimum.

  2. 02

    Roast in small batches

    Twelve kilos at a time, profiled for each lot and cupped before it ships.

  3. 03

    Ship within 48 hours

    Your coffee leaves the roastery two days after roasting, at most.
