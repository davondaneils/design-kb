# Brief: 04 Micro-interactions

**Question it answers:** What are the exact specs for the small moments: press, hover, toggle, tooltip, popover, toast, loading, success, error, drag?

**After reading, a designer can decide:** the property, distance, scale, duration and easing for each common component, plus trigger, rules, feedback and loops (Saffer's model).

**Must cover:** button press scale; hover gating for touch; tooltip delay and skip delay; origin-aware popovers; toasts; skeletons vs spinners and response time limits (0.1 s, 1 s, 10 s); error shake; success; number and icon swaps; gestures (respond immediately, commit on release, momentum projection, rubber banding).

**Test briefs:** add-to-cart feedback; a settings toggle; a hold-to-delete control.

**Hand-offs:** design-ux (states), design-copy (labels in feedback), design-graphic (icon animation).
