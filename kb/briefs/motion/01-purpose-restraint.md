# Brief: 01 Purpose and restraint

**Question it answers:** Should this element move at all, and if so, what job is the motion doing?

**After reading, a designer can decide:**
- Whether an interaction gets motion, a reduced version, or none, based on its job and how often it is seen.
- Which of the five motion jobs (feedback, state change, spatial orientation, signifier, attention) a proposed animation serves, and reject it if none.
- Where the brand is allowed to be expressive (rare moments) versus productive (task flows).

**Must cover:** NN/g purposes of animation; frequency ladder (100+ per day = none); keyboard-initiated actions never animate; productive vs expressive (Carbon); motion as a dark pattern; Apple "let people cancel motion".

**Test briefs it should pass:** command palette for a dev tool (answer: no open animation); onboarding celebration for a fitness app (answer: expressive, once, skippable).

**Hand-offs:** design-ux (flows, feedback states), design-copy (empty and success states), website-strategy (brand personality feeds the expressive budget).
