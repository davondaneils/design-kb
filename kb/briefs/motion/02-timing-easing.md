# Brief: 02 Timing, easing and springs

**Question it answers:** How long, and on what curve?

**After reading, a designer can decide:** a duration token for any element by size, distance, frequency and direction; an easing token by enter, exit, on-screen move or constant motion; when to switch from a bezier to a spring and with what parameters.

**Must cover:** shared foundation tokens (`--duration-instant` to `--duration-page`, `--easing-standard`, `--easing-enter`, `--easing-exit`); NN/g 100 to 500 ms band; Material 1 enter vs exit asymmetry and device scaling; Material 3 tokens and springs (spatial vs effects); Carbon productive vs expressive; Apple damping and response; Motion (Framer Motion) spring options; `linear()` for springs in CSS; why ease-in feels slow for UI.

**Test briefs:** modal and drawer timing spec; a drag-to-dismiss sheet with flick.

**Hand-offs:** design-frontend (token files), design-ux (gesture design).
