# Brief 01: Logo and identity

**Question:** How do I design, test and specify a mark and the identity system around it?

**After reading, a designer can decide:** wordmark vs monogram vs symbol vs combination; when a mark is done (stress tests passed); which lockups and versions to ship; clear space and minimum sizes; how the logo relates to the rest of the identity (type, color, imagery, layout devices).

**Must cover**
- What a logo does and does not do (Rand; Chermayeff and Geismar and Haviv).
- Mark type decision rules tied to brand stage, name length and exposure budget.
- Process: brief, audit of the competitive field, sketch volume, build on a grid, correct optically.
- Stress tests: 16px, one color, reversed, blur and squint, competitor wall, small print, signage or embroidery where relevant.
- Optical corrections: overshoot, irradiation in reversed versions, optical centering.
- Lockups, clear space unit, minimum sizes in px and mm, color versions.
- Identity beyond the mark: the few assets that carry recognition.
- Presentation: in context, at real size, with rationale.

**Test briefs it should pass**
- A new specialty coffee roaster needs a mark and starter identity kit.
- A SaaS rebrand where the old logo fails as a favicon.
