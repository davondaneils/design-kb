# Brief 03: Photography and image direction

**Question:** How do I direct, select, crop and treat photography so it informs and stays on brand?

**After reading, a designer can decide:** whether a photo earns its place; what a shot brief must specify; how to pick stock without cliché; crops and focal points across breakpoints; treatments (grade, duotone) for consistency; text over images; rules for AI generated imagery.

**Must cover**
- NN/g evidence: informative images get attention, filler is ignored, real people beat stock models, enlargements at least 2x.
- Art direction brief fields: subject, moment, light, lens, angle, palette, grade, negative space for copy.
- Selection and edit rules for a consistent set.
- Text over image contrast with scrims.
- AI imagery: disclosure, provenance (C2PA), defect checks, rights, no fabricated customers.

**Test briefs it should pass**
- Hero and supporting imagery direction for a fintech landing page.
