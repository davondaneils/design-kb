# Brief 04: Illustration

**Question:** When is illustration the right choice, and how do I specify a style that many people (and AI tools) can reproduce consistently?

**After reading, a designer can decide:** photo vs illustration vs icon vs nothing; illustration tiers and their detail budgets; a style spec (palette, line, light, perspective, character rules); dark mode variants; accessibility treatment.

**Must cover**
- Design system practice: Atlassian (spot, low-fi UI, ambient), Dropbox (hero, spot, mini; illustration for abstract concepts, photography for real-world proof).
- Style spec fields and a worked spec.
- Failure modes: generic corporate flat people, style drift between illustrators, illustration replacing the message.

**Test briefs it should pass**
- Empty, error and onboarding illustrations for a B2B product.
