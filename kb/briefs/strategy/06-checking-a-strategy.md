# Brief 06: Checking a strategy

## Question
How do I prove, before shipping, that the strategy is distinctive, clear and true, using tests a single builder can run?

## Audience
Claude in the self-critique loop, and the critic.

## Decisions the reader must be able to make
- Run the word swap test and the competitor wall (first screens of named competitors beside ours).
- Run hide-the-logo and the five-second test with a cold viewer (a fresh-context subagent counts as one).
- Decide when a real message test (Wynter style) is worth it and how many responses.
- Build the claims ledger: claim, fact, source, evidence held.
- For identity work: test recognition, not liking.

## Fundamentals
Own judgment never counts as a cold viewer. Every claim traced. Results recorded in the hand-over.

## Expert layer
Recognition over liking (Caruso et al. 2025 via the 2026 benchmark); message-test saturation at 12 to 13 responses; testing the hero line, the visual and the claims separately.

## Must cover
Five-second test (Perfetti), message testing (Wynter), CAP 3.7 and FTC substantiation, design-director step 11.

## Out of scope or reuse
Concept legibility check (design-playbook-brand 2a); `claims_inventory.mjs` mechanics (design-copy).

## Test briefs
Reuse the brief 03 and 05 test pages.

## Rubric
Every test has a recorded result; any fail has a named fix.
