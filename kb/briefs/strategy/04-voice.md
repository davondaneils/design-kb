# Brief 04: Voice

## Question
How does the brand sound, written so the copy skills and the visual system can both use it, without duplicating the two voice skills?

## Audience
Claude filling the voice line of the strategy brief.

## Decisions the reader must be able to make
- Pick 3 to 4 voice attributes as "this, not that" pairs, each with one sample line in the brand's real context.
- Take the words from the audience's own language (brief 02).
- Mark the moments where the voice goes flat (errors, money, security), from design-copy's tone map.
- Hand each attribute to its visual twin in the identity table (brief 05).
- Know when to run crafting-voice-identity in full (a voice guide is a deliverable) and when the four lines are enough.

## Fundamentals
Attributes as pairs with samples, never adjectives alone. Clarity outranks personality. No humor in errors, money loss, security.

## Expert layer
One attribute set drives words and visuals; a competitor tonality check; banned words from the category.

## Must cover
Mailchimp "same voice, tone changes"; the adapter table to crafting-voice-identity and design-copy 03.

## Out of scope or reuse
Voice chart, tone map, mechanics (design-copy 03). Voice Identity Spectrum, Polarity Principle, scenarios (crafting-voice-identity).

## Test briefs
1. A calm meditation app. 2. A challenger bank.

## Rubric
Each attribute has a boundary and a sample line; a visual twin exists for each.
