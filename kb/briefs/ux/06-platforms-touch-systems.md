# Brief: Platforms, touch and design systems

## Question it answers
How do we respect iOS, Android and web conventions, design for fingers, and build components that stay consistent?

## After reading, a designer can decide
- Target sizes and placement per platform and per screen zone.
- Modal vs sheet vs popover vs full screen; alert vs action sheet.
- Which platform convention wins when a brand wants something custom.
- What every component must specify (anatomy, states, behavior, accessibility, content rules).
- How tokens and components relate (hand-off to design-frontend and the shared foundations contract).

## Must cover
- Apple HIG: buttons 44x44 pt, tab bars, sheets and detents, modality, alerts, privacy.
- Android: 48x48 dp targets, window size classes, adaptive navigation, Material state layers.
- Hoober touch research (grips, center preference, 7 mm to 12 mm).
- WAI-ARIA APG patterns, starting with dialog.
- Design tokens (DTCG 2025.10), atomic design.

## Test briefs it should pass
- "Port our iOS app's navigation to Android and web."
- "Spec a button component for our design system."
