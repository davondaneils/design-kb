# Brief 06: Local SEO basics and measurement

Owner hat: domain-specialist (search). Status: drafting. Brief date: 2026-10-09.

## Question it answers
How does a local business win the map pack and local organic, and how does any site measure search and AI search performance honestly?

## After reading, a designer must be able to decide
- Google Business Profile setup: name, categories, service area vs storefront, hours.
- Location and service page structure for local sites.
- Which reports to read (Search Console Performance, branded filter, Generative AI report, Bing AI Performance, GA4 custom AI channel).
- What KPIs to report and what not to promise.

## Must cover
- Google local ranking: relevance, distance, prominence; no paying for rank.
- GBP name rules (no keywords), category rules, SAB rules, virtual office ineligibility.
- Whitespark 2026 LSRF top factors (survey opinion, labeled).
- LocalBusiness schema fit with GBP.
- GA4 AI channel regex and caveats; AI Overview clicks look like google/organic.
- Search Console Generative AI performance report (June 2026, impressions only, UK first) and opt-out toggle in testing.
