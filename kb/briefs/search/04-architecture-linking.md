# Brief 04: Site architecture, URLs and internal linking

Owner hat: domain-specialist (search). Status: drafting. Brief date: 2026-10-09.

## Question it answers
How should pages be organized, addressed and linked so that crawlers find them, authority flows to money pages, and users and machines understand the topic map?

## After reading, a designer must be able to decide
- Navigation depth and hub (pillar) structure for a site of any size.
- URL patterns, including faceted filters, pagination and parameters.
- Where internal links go and what their anchor text says.
- Subfolder vs subdomain vs ccTLD (opinion plus Google guidance).

## Must cover
- Google link rules: `<a href>`, descriptive anchors, every important page linked at least once.
- Faceted navigation (Google, Dec 2025 doc): block or make crawlable, `&` separator, 404 for empty combos.
- Pagination: rel next/prev unused by Google, self-canonical per page, crawlable links for infinite scroll.
- URL rules: hyphens, lowercase, readable words, no fragments for content.
- Click depth and orphan detection as a practice (practitioner consensus).
