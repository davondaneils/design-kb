# Brief: Voice and tone systems

**Domain:** copy. **Skill:** design-copy.

## Question
How do you define a product voice so that ten writers (or one AI) produce copy that sounds like one brand, and how does tone shift by moment?

## Decisions the reader must be able to make
- The 3 or 4 voice attributes, each with a "this, not that" boundary.
- Where the voice sits on the four NN/g tone dimensions.
- How tone shifts for the reader's emotional state (success, error, money, loss, legal).
- Mechanics: case, contractions, punctuation, numbers, person.

## Fundamentals (non-negotiable)
- Clarity beats personality every time; voice never costs comprehension.
- Voice is constant; tone varies with the reader's state.
- No humor in errors, money loss, security or legal moments.
- One mechanics rule set applied everywhere.

## Expert layer
- The Podmajersky voice chart (attributes as columns, concepts/vocabulary/verbosity/grammar/punctuation/capitalization as rows).
- Tone maps by moment, with a dial per dimension.
- Resolving conflicts between published guides (contractions, exclamation marks).
- Voice of customer mining as the vocabulary source.

## Must cover
Voice attributes, tone dimensions, tone map, mechanics sheet, word list, examples, how to brief an AI or a writer.

## Out of scope or reuse
Brand strategy and positioning: `website-strategy`. Brand voice discovery workshops: the user's crafting-voice-identity skill if present.
