# Brief: Contrast and accessibility (WCAG 2.2, APCA as draft)

**Domain:** color. **Skill:** design-color. **Audience:** a designer building websites and apps for themselves and for clients.

## Question
What contrast is required, what is merely recommended, and how do you design so you pass without thinking about it?

## Decisions the reader must be able to make
- Which ratio applies to a given element (body text, large text, icons, borders, focus rings, disabled, placeholder, logos).
- When to use APCA and how to label it.
- How to handle color blindness and forced colors.

## Fundamentals (non-negotiable)
- 4.5:1 body text, 3:1 large text and UI parts, no rounding.
- Never color alone.
- Visible focus with 3:1 against adjacent colors.

## Expert layer
- Where WCAG 2 and APCA disagree (dark mode, white on mid blues and oranges) and what to do about it.
- Lightness thresholds that pass by construction.
- Legal baselines (WCAG 2.1 AA in ADA Title II and EN 301 549) vs best practice (2.2 AA).

## Must cover
SC 1.4.1, 1.4.3, 1.4.6, 1.4.11, 2.4.7, 2.4.11, 2.4.13; WCAG luminance formula; APCA Lc levels; WCAG 3 status; color vision deficiency prevalence; forced-colors.

## Test briefs
- "Is light gray placeholder text OK?"
- "Check this dark mode: #3b82f6 links on #111."
