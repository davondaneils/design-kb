# Brief: Color psychology and brand

**Domain:** color. **Skill:** design-color. **Audience:** a designer building websites and apps for themselves and for clients.

## Question
What does the evidence actually say about color and emotion, and how should a designer pick brand color without folklore?

## Decisions the reader must be able to make
- Which color claims to trust, which to treat as convention, and which to drop.
- How to choose a brand hue for a client using category, competitors and ownability.
- How to present color choices to a client.

## Fundamentals (non-negotiable)
- Separate evidence, convention, and folklore every time.
- Context and culture change meaning.

## Expert layer
- The red effect literature and its limits; the uncited "62 to 90 percent" figure.
- Brand personality links (Labrecque and Milne) as weak, context-bound signals.
- Ownability and category conventions beat "blue means trust".

## Must cover
Elliot (2015) review, Singh (2006), Labrecque and Milne (2012), Apple HIG on cultural meaning. Reuse website-strategy for positioning.

## Test briefs
- "What color should my client's wellness brand be? I read green means health."
