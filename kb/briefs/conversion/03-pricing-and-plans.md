# Brief: 03 Pricing and plan presentation

**Question:** How do I present a price, a choice of plans or cadences, and the terms, so the right choice is easy and nothing is hidden?

**Audience:** Claude as art director on a pricing page, a subscription section, or a product card.

**Decisions the reader must be able to make**
- What goes in the price block: amount, unit, what is included, billing period, next charge, how to cancel.
- How many plans, which is recommended, how the recommendation is shown and preselected.
- Annual vs monthly: what to show as the headline number and how to state the billed amount.
- Discounts: percent or currency, strike-through or not, and how to check the math.
- Comparison tables: when, how many columns, which rows.

**Fundamentals (gate)**
- Total cost visible before commitment: shipping, tax treatment and renewal (Baymard: 40% abandon on extra costs, 12% on not seeing total; UK DMCC Act drip pricing ban, April 2025).
- Subscription terms at the point of consent, clear and conspicuous (California ARL as amended, July 2025; ROSCA).
- Every number on the page is correct: savings computed, units shown, per-unit prices comparable.
- Prices use tabular figures and the units are real characters (design-typography 05).

**Expert layer**
- The price block as one typeset unit, read as a sentence.
- Defaults over decoys: preselect the recommended option with a stated reason; decoy tiers mostly fail outside numeric lab tasks (Frederick, Lee and Baskin 2014).
- Savings in currency for small prices, percent only when it is exact and large.
- "Billed yearly" next to a per-month figure, every time (Linear pricing).

**Must cover:** price block anatomy, plan card anatomy, cadence selector, toggle, comparison table, math check, legal floor by region.

**Out of scope or reuse:** price setting and offer stacks (sales-funnels), positioning (website-strategy), pricing table markup (design-product-ui 01 for dense tables).

**Test briefs**
1. Coffee subscription: two cadences, one or two bags, shipping included. Playbook landing. Exemplar: apple.com/shop buy pages.
2. SaaS pricing page, three plans plus enterprise, annual toggle. Playbook landing. Exemplar: linear.app/pricing, vercel.com/pricing.

**Rubric:** gate first. Then: price block answers amount, unit, included, cadence, next charge, cancel; recommended option preselected with a reason; every saving recomputed by the tester and correct.
